# LeeScoop Editorial Taste Brief

Use this as the gut-check before selecting stories or rewriting titles.

## Core vibe
LeeScoop should feel alive, local, and worth checking every day.

Not a sleepy bulletin.
Not a chamber-of-commerce brochure.
Not a city meeting transcript farm.

## What to prioritize
### News
Pick stories that make a local reader stop scrolling:
- police, sheriff, raids, arrests, major crime, public safety operations
- fires, crashes, outages, boil notices, evacuations, missing persons, storm impacts
- development fights, big growth moves, housing, traffic, beach recovery, school shakeups
- corruption, scams, fraud, lawsuits, political conflict with real local impact
- weird or unusually talkable local stories

### Events
Pick events that make people say:
- “wait, that’s happening here?”
- “I didn’t know that was coming”
- “we should go to that”

Prefer:
- major touring acts
- big festivals
- citywide happenings
- visually striking or unusual experiences
- obvious weekend anchors

The daily feed is event-first. Discover across Lee County without city quotas; prioritize useful near-term options. Keep selected event picks varied by source, venue, date, audience, and event type; LeeScoop should not read like an RSS feed for Hertz Arena and Barbara B. Mann.

Avoid using too many slots on tiny filler events if stronger events exist.

## Headline style
Headlines should be:
- factual
- specific
- punchy
- hook-forward

They should not be:
- flat
- bureaucratic
- sanitized into bland civic language

### Better headline patterns
- lead with the strongest real hook
- keep the local angle visible
- preserve source energy when truthful
- shorten only if the hook survives

## Images
### News
Default to source images from the original article/page.
- Prefer `og:image` or the main article image.
- Use obvious first-party publisher/CDN images when they clearly belong to the outlet.
- No AI art by default; missing source images require an explicit editorial decision, not automatic generation.

### Events
Use coastal LeeScoop-style cover art only through verified Codex OAuth (openai/gpt-image-2); no API-key or ComfyUI fallback. Preserve existing covers. Incomplete covers block publishing.

## Quick reject test
If a pick feels like one of these, reject it unless the day is dead quiet:
- generic civic process update
- mild feature with no real stakes
- small recurring event with no headline pull
- anything that reads like filler content
