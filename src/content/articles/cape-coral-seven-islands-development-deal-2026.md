## What happened?

WINK News reports Cape Coral's Seven Islands project is moving closer to reality as council considers a development agreement for the 48-acre site along Old Burnt Store Road. City leaders say the plan has been shaped over several years and could bring restaurants, businesses, and other amenities to northwest Cape Coral. If approved, the property could close as early as next month after permits move forward.
