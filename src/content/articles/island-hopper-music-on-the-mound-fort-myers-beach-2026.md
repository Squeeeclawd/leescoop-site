## What is it?

The Town of Fort Myers Beach lists Music on the Mound for Saturday, September 26, from 2 to 4 p.m. at Mound House. The $50 event features two songwriters as part of Island Hopper Songwriters Fest.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
