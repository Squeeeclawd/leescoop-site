## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers lists Monthly Music Walk for Friday, July 17, from 6 to 10 p.m. in the downtown River District. The city says local and regional musicians line the streets, the lineup changes each month, and the event is free to attend.

## Who is it for?

Downtown walkers, live-music fans, families, and anyone looking for a free Friday night
