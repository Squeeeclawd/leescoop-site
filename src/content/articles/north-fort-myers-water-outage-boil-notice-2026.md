## What happened?

WINK reports Lee County Utilities scheduled a water outage starting at 9 a.m. Tuesday for 1,604 residences and 22 commercial sites in North Fort Myers. The interruption is tied to installation of a 12-inch valve and pipe replacement work. Residents are expected to be without water for up to eight hours, followed by a precautionary boil-water notice until testing clears the system.
