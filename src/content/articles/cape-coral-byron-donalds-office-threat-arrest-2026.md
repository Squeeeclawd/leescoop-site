## What happened?

Gulf Coast News reported that Lee County deputies arrested Daniel Eberly after a tip about threatening calls to Rep. Byron Donalds' office in Cape Coral. Sheriff Carmine Marceno said the suspect threatened to blow up the office and 'shoot every cop.' The suspect was located at his Cape Coral home and booked on charges tied to a false weapon-of-mass-destruction report and threats against a public servant.
