## What is it?

Alliance for the Arts lists Boos & Booze for Halloween night as part of Stage Fright Nights. The free movie side event starts with The Curse of Bridge Hollow at 7 p.m. and follows with Halloween at 8:30 p.m., with concessions and a seasonal cocktail available. The haunted-house portion is separate and paid, while the movie event does not require a haunted-house ticket.

## Who is it for?

Halloween fans, movie-night groups, arts-center regulars, and adults looking for a spooky-but-social holiday plan
