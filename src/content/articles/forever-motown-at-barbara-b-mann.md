## What is it?

Forever Motown is a live tribute show built around classic Motown songs and performers. The show features veteran entertainers, a live band, and nearly 30 hits associated with artists like The Temptations, The Four Tops, The Supremes, Smokey Robinson, Marvin Gaye, Stevie Wonder, and more.

## Who is it for?

This is a good fit for anyone who wants a polished live music night, especially people who grew up with Motown or just want a familiar, upbeat show.
