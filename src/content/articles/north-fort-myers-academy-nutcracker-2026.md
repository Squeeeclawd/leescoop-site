## What is it?

North Fort Myers Academy for the Arts brings its two-act production of The Nutcracker to Barbara B. Mann Performing Arts Hall on Friday, December 4, at 7 p.m. The venue says more than 200 students ages 6–14 will perform Tchaikovsky’s holiday ballet. Tickets start at $22 including processing fees. This is the academy’s 24th year producing the show and its 16th performance at Barbara B. Mann.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
