## What is it?

Alliance for the Arts lists Alliance Morning Market for the third Saturday of each month from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. The Fort Myers campus event includes local vendors, artists, live entertainment, Family ArtLab, and a free family movie. Admission is free, with a suggested donation supporting Alliance arts programming.

## Who is it for?

Families, art shoppers, and anyone looking for a free Saturday morning plan.
