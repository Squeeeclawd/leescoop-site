## What happened?

A judge denied Thomas Stein's request for a new trial after his May 8 conviction on first-degree felony murder and three attempted robbery counts. The Lee County State Attorney's Office confirmed the ruling, and court records show Stein still faces a possible life sentence. His sentencing remains scheduled for July 10 after defense arguments over alleged discovery and trial errors failed to overturn the verdict.
