## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists Captivaville Songwriters Weekend for June 13 and 14 at Tween Waters Inn & Marina on Captiva. The event promises poolside sets from five local artists, plus day-pass and overnight-guest access to a sunny resort setting. It has the kind of breezy, shareable summer-weekend energy Lee County readers actually click.

## Who is it for?

Music fans, visitors, and locals looking for a laid-back island weekend event.
