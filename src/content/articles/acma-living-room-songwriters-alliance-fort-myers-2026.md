## What is it?

The Americana Community Music Association is bringing Hank Woji and Roy Schneider to Alliance for the Arts on August 29. The two veteran songwriters will perform original music alongside songs that influenced them, while sharing stories about their creative process. Doors open at 6:30 p.m. and the concert starts at 7 p.m.

## Who is it for?

Americana fans, songwriter listeners, and anyone who prefers an intimate story-driven concert
