## What happened?

WINK reports a Lehigh Acres man was accused of firing at a Lee County Sheriff's Office drone while deputies were using it during a bear response. The incident went viral and triggered broader questions about privacy, firearms and what is legal when drones fly over homes. The station notes FAA rules govern airspace once a drone leaves the ground, even when it is flying over private property.
