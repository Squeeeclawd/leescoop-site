## What happened?

WINK reported that overnight closures begin at 8 p.m. Monday at the I-75 and Daniels Parkway interchange while crews work on overhead signals. Both eastbound and westbound Daniels Parkway are expected to see brief full closures, and nearby I-75 ramps may also shut down temporarily. Treeline Avenue and Six Mile Cypress Parkway are the suggested detours, making this an immediate commuter-impact story.
