## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers calendar lists Monthly Music Walk for Friday, May 15, from 6 to 10 p.m. Local and regional musicians line the streets of downtown Fort Myers, and the event is free to attend. It is a recurring River District night out built around live music and walkable blocks.

## Who is it for?

All ages
