## What happened?

Fort Myers Beach Talk reports a pregnant adult manatee struck in Matanzas Pass died two days later at ZooTampa from blunt-force trauma. The area is a manatee protection zone with minimum-wake limits, and officials said Lee County has already recorded 85 manatee deaths this year, including 13 directly tied to watercraft collisions. The case adds more pressure on boaters in one of the county's busiest and most sensitive waterways.
