## What happened?

Downtown Fort Myers reopened after a bomb threat triggered evacuations and road closures on Sunday afternoon. Fort Myers police later said the threat was a swatting call, meaning a false emergency report designed to trigger a major response. The hoax temporarily displaced downtown workers and residents while officers cleared the area. Police said the investigation remains active as they work to trace where the call came from.
