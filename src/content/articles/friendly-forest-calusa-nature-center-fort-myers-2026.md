## What is it?

Friendly Forest returns to Calusa Nature Center and Planetarium for a two-day fall festival on September 19 and 20. The family lineup includes a pumpkin patch, photo spots, games, scavenger hunts, crafts, a bounce house, animal talks, and planetarium shows. Festival hours are 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. each day.

## Who is it for?

Families with children, nature-center regulars, and anyone ready for an early fall outing
