## What happened?

WINK News reported that the large sandbar off Blind Pass has cleared, reopening water flow between Sanibel and Captiva. The blockage had been a notable local concern since spring because officials tied it to the unstable post-storm coastal conditions around the pass. The update matters for island residents, boaters, and beach watchers following shoreline recovery and renourishment work.
