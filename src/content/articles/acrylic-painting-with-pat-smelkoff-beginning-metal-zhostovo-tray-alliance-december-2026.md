## What is it?

Pat Smelkoff’s beginning series explores Zhostovo, a Russian decorative painting tradition known for hand-painted metal trays. The December 3 session runs four hours. This is a multi-week series, so confirm the registration commitment and supply list rather than assuming it is a drop-in class.

[Official event details](https://www.artinlee.org/event/acrylic-painting-with-pat-smelkoff-beginning-metal-zhostovo-tray/2026-12-03/).

## Who is it for?

Artists interested in traditional decorative painting on metal.
