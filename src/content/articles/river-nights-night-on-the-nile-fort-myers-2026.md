## What is it?

YMCA of Southwest Florida lists River Nights for Friday, September 25, starting at 6 p.m. at Sidney and Berne Davis Art Center. The fundraiser is billed as A Night on the Nile, with an elegant ancient-Egypt-inspired evening supporting YMCA programs and scholarship work.

## Who is it for?

Fundraiser supporters, downtown night-out planners, arts-center regulars, and people who like themed gala events
