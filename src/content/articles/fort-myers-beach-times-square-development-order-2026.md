## What happened?

The Fort Myers Beach Observer reports town staff issued a development order for Terry Persaud's proposed mixed-use entertainment center at 1028 and 1046 Estero Boulevard. The project now calls for six hotel units, a restaurant, shopping, services, entertainment space, a rooftop bar, and a pool while staying within current code instead of seeking the larger deviations that drew pushback last year. It is one of the more visible redevelopment moves yet in the heart of Times Square after Hurricane Ian.
