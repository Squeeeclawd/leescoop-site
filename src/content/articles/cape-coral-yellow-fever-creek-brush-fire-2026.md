## What happened?

Cape Coral and North Fort Myers fire crews responded to a brush fire burning at Yellow Fever Creek Preserve. WINK reported the fire covered about five acres and was nearly contained as crews worked the scene with the Florida Forest Service. Officials said no homes were in danger. The incident still drew attention because of the preserve location and the size of the fire during summer heat.
