## What happened?

Breeze Airways said travelers at Southwest Florida International Airport will get three new routes to Dayton, Madison, and Trenton. WINK reports the Dayton and Madison nonstop flights start in October, while Trenton begins in January. Introductory fares start at $69 one way for some routes, with Trenton starting at $89. It is a tangible travel and tourism update for Lee County flyers heading into the fall season.
