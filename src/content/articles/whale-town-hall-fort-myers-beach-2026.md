## What is it?

Mayor Dan Allers and Town Manager Will McKannay are scheduled to host a community discussion Thursday, October 15, from 5 to 7 p.m. at The Whale. The town announcement says the conversation will address where the town stands after this year’s budget process.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
