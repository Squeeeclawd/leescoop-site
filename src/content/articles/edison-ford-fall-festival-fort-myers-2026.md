## What is it?

Edison and Ford Winter Estates lists its Fall Festival for October 31 from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. The official page describes craft vendor booths, food and drinks, music and paid activities, with free admission to attend the festival grounds.

## Who is it for?

Families, shoppers, garden lovers and anyone looking for a daytime Halloween outing.
