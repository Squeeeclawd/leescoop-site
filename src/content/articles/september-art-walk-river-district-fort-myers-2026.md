## What is it?

The Fort Myers River District lists September Art Walk for Friday, Sept. 4, from 5 to 9 p.m. The free downtown event features more than 60 artists showcasing, demonstrating and selling work along the historic streets. The listing describes it as family-friendly and part of a nearly 19-year Fort Myers tradition.

## Who is it for?

Families, art shoppers, downtown walkers and date-night planners.
