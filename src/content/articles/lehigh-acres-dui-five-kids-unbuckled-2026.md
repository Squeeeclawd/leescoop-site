## What happened?

Gulf Coast News reports Florida Highway Patrol arrested a 30-year-old driver after a Lehigh Acres traffic stop on SR-82 and Gateway Boulevard. Troopers said she could not maintain her lane, showed signs of impairment, and admitted to drinking and smoking marijuana. FHP said five children were inside the vehicle, four were not wearing seatbelts, and a two-year-old was sitting on the lap of another unrestrained child.
