## What is it?

The Fort Myers Beach Chamber lists the first Coastal Cars & Coffee Summer Series event for Saturday morning at Santini Marina Plaza, and the opening theme is not subtle: exotic and supercar showcase.

The listing says guests can expect Lamborghinis, Ferraris, McLarens, Porsches, and other high-performance vehicles around the plaza.

## Why this one is easy to sell

It is free, short, beach-adjacent, and built for people who want something more visually ridiculous than another normal Saturday morning. The event is also tied to Fast Lane Drive Naples, Lamborghini Club America Florida members, and the Porsche Club of America Everglades Region.

After the showcase, participating vehicles are expected to cruise together to The Whale for lunch and reserved parking.

## Quick details

- **When:** Saturday, May 23, 2026, 9-11 a.m.
- **Where:** Santini Marina Plaza, 7205 Estero Blvd.
- **Cost:** Free for spectators
- **What to expect:** Exotic cars, luxury cars, supercar-club energy, and a post-show cruise to The Whale
