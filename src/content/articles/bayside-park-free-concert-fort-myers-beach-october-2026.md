## What is it?

Bayside Park’s free outdoor concert series has an October 4 date on Fort Myers Beach, running 4–7 p.m. Bring a lawn chair for local live music; well-behaved pets on leashes six feet or shorter are welcome. No outside food or beverages are permitted, and the concert is weather-dependent. Check the official listing before heading out.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
