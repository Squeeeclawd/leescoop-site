## What happened?

Cape Coral officials lifted the temporary burn ban issued in January after the Keetch-Byram Drought Index fell below 600. Residents can resume recreational and cooking fires, but city burn rules still apply. Fires must stay at least 25 feet from structures and cannot be used to burn vegetation or rubbish.
