## What is it?

Charlie’s Cards & Collectibles is bringing vendors, collectibles and family-friendly shopping to Edison Mall on Saturday, October 17, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Admission is free. The mall is at 4125 Cleveland Avenue in Fort Myers. The Cape Coral Breeze lists the event as open to the public; check the organizer for any updates before heading over.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
