## What is it?

Calusa Nature Center & Planetarium illuminates its decorated Pine Loop Trail on selected December evenings. Holiday Lights runs 6–9 p.m. on December 4–6, 11–13, 18–20 and 26–27 at 3450 Ortiz Avenue in Fort Myers. Choose a dated ticket through the event page and confirm holiday admission before visiting.
