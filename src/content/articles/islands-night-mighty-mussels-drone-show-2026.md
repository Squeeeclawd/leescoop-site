## What is it?

The SanCap Chamber says Islands Night is set for May 8 when the Fort Myers Mighty Mussels face the Tampa Tarpons. The event includes a pre-game parade showcasing Sanibel and Captiva groups, with gates opening at 5:45 p.m. and the parade starting at 5:50 p.m. The chamber is also promoting Fort Myers’ first-ever drone show as part of the night.

## Who is it for?

Island residents, families, and baseball fans
