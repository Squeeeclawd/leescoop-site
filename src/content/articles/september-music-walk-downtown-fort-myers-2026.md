## What is it?

The Fort Myers River District lists September Music Walk for Friday, Sept. 18, from 6 to 10 p.m. Historic downtown closes to welcome bands, DJs, soloists, duets, dancing and other live performances. The monthly third-Friday event is free, with the day's performer list posted separately by the organizer.

## Who is it for?

Downtown music fans, walkers, groups and visitors looking for a free night out.
