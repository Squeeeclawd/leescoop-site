## What is it?

Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center is hosting Making the Portrait: A Masterclass in Light & Presence on November 8. The full-day class covers one-light portraits, clamshell and rim lighting, directing subjects, mixed light, and live critique while participants rotate through stations with professional models. The venue says only 20 spots are available.

## Who is it for?

Photographers comfortable with manual mode who want hands-on portrait and lighting practice
