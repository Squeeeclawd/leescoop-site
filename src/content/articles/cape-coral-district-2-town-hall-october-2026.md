## What is it?

District 2 Council Member Laurie Lehmann is hosting a public town hall at Rotary Park. Residents can meet neighbors, raise concerns, ask questions, and discuss ideas for District 2 and Cape Coral directly with their council member.

## Who is it for?

Cape Coral residents who want to ask questions, share concerns, or discuss District 2 and city issues.
