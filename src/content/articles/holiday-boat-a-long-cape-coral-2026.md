## What is it?

Cape Coral’s Holiday Boat-a-Long returns to Four Freedoms Park on December 19, 4–9 p.m. The free family event includes a boat parade, stage entertainment, food trucks, crafts and a visit from Santa. A movie follows the parade. Bring lawn chairs or blankets; check the city’s page for boat registration details.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.

Use [City of Cape Coral](https://www.capecoral.gov/community/special_events/holiday_boat_a_long.php) for current details, tickets, policies, and schedule changes.
