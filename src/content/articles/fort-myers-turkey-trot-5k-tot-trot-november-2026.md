## What is it?

Fort Myers Turkey Trot starts Thanksgiving morning, November 26, with a 5K run/walk at 7:30 a.m. The course starts on McGregor Boulevard and finishes at Edison and Ford Winter Estates. The Tot Trot for ages six and younger follows at approximately 8:45 a.m. Register by November 25 at 11:59 p.m.; there is no race-day registration. McGregor Boulevard closes at 6 a.m., so check the parking map and arrive early. Confirm current 5K pricing at registration; the Tot Trot is $10.
