## What happened?

WINK News reports Cape Coral council approved a mobility fee plan after revisiting the proposal later in the same meeting. The fees are meant to help address a projected infrastructure shortfall over the next 20 years. Under the ordinance, fees rise annually starting in 2026 and must be spent within the districts where they are collected.
