## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers lists October's Monthly Music Walk for Friday, October 16. Local and regional musicians will perform along First Street, with styles ranging from jazz and blues to rock and roll. The free downtown event runs from 6 to 10 p.m.

## Who is it for?

Live-music fans, downtown walkers, date-night planners, and anyone looking for a free Friday outing
