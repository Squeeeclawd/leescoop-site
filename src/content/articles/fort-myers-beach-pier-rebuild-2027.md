## What happened?

WINK News reports the Fort Myers Beach Pier rebuild is expected to start in summer 2026 once federal permits are approved, with a potential completion in summer 2027. Lee County is handling the rebuild as part of wider hurricane recovery work. Renderings show a longer, wider pier with ADA access, seating, and turtle-friendly lighting.
