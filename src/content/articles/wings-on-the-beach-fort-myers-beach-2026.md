## What is it?

What's Up FMB lists the inaugural Wings on the Beach for Saturday, Oct. 3, from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. Passport holders can visit 12 participating Fort Myers Beach restaurants, sample three signature wings at each stop and vote for a People's Choice champion. The $25 passport also includes a crawl map, awards celebration and raffle entry.

## Who is it for?

Food fans, beach visitors and groups ready for a full-day crawl.
