## What happened?

The News-Press reports that polls are open from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Tuesday for Florida's primary election. In Lee County, voters are deciding a District 5 county commission race, two school board seats, U.S. House primaries, Cape Coral municipal contests, and other regional races. The Lee County Elections page verifies the Aug. 18 polling window and vote-by-mail return deadline.
