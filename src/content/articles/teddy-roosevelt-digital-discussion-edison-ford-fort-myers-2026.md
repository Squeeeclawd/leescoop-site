## What is it?

Edison and Ford Winter Estates lists Digital Discussion: Teddy Roosevelt in War and Peace for Tuesday, Aug. 25, at 10:30 a.m. The registered online program covers Roosevelt's life before, during and after his presidency and points to a Southwest Florida connection. The presentation is supported by the Rotary Club of Fort Myers.

## Who is it for?

History fans, museum-program regulars and online learners.
