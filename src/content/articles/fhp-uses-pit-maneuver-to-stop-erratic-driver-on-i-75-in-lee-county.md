## What happened?

WINK reports Florida Highway Patrol arrested a driver on Interstate 75 in Lee County after the vehicle was reported driving erratically and failing to maintain its lane. Troopers say the driver refused a traffic stop, forcing them to use a PIT maneuver near Mile Marker 125. The driver was taken to jail and faces DUI and fleeing charges.
