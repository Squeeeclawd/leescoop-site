GLITCHHOUSE is scheduled for Friday, September 5, at 1920 Honda Drive in Fort Myers, with doors at 7 p.m. and music running until 2 a.m. Organizers list a $15 entry, 18-plus admission, free parking, vendors, hot food and live art.
