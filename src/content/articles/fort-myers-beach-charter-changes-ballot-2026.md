## What happened?

The Fort Myers Beach Observer reports town council approved charter amendments for the November ballot after debate over appointed councilmember terms and a proposed salary commission. Voters will decide each question individually, including changes tied to mayoral succession, election qualifying, and gift and ex parte rules. The package could reshape how the town fills vacancies and governs itself after a politically tense year.
