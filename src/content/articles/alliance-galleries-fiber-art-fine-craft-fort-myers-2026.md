## What is it?

Alliance for the Arts lists its August and September galleries as on display through Saturday, Sept. 26. The exhibition brings together fiber art, fine craft, mixed media, jewelry, glass, sculpture and atmospheric work built through layers of paint and mixed media. The listing gives Tuesday-through-Friday gallery hours of 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. and Saturday hours of 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.

## Who is it for?

Gallery visitors, artists, photographers and craft fans.
