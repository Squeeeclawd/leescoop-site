## What happened?

Jaycee Park has officially reopened after a major renovation in Cape Coral.

## What’s new?

The updated park includes a lighted riverfront boardwalk, improved pathways, native landscaping, better accessibility, an inclusive playground, adult changing stations, a 5,000-square-foot splash pad, shaded picnic areas, a bandshell, games, volleyball courts, and a future food truck court.

## What’s next?

A public ribbon cutting is scheduled for Friday, May 8, at 10 AM.
