## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists Bonita Riverfest for Saturday, August 1, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. The revived downtown event centers on the Imperial River with canoe, kayak, paddleboard, homemade-vessel, and rubber duck races, plus food, shopping, live entertainment, and family activities.

## Who is it for?

Families, paddlers, river watchers, and downtown Bonita visitors.
