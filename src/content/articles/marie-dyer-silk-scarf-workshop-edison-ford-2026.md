## What is it?

Artist Marie Dyer leads a silk scarf painting class in the gardens at 2350 McGregor Boulevard. No painting experience is required. Materials are included in the $100 member or $110 nonmember rate. Space is limited, with registration through the estates; December 13 is the next date listed after November 29.
