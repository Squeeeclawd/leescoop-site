## What is it?

Florida Repertory Theatre opens The Queen of Bingo on May 27 at the Artstage Studio Theatre in downtown Fort Myers. The comedy follows two sisters chasing fun through bingo, family drama, and a deliberately goofy stage setup. Florida Rep also promises a real bingo game during the show, with one audience member winning a frozen turkey each performance.

## Who is it for?

Theatergoers, comedy fans, and anyone who likes interactive live shows.
