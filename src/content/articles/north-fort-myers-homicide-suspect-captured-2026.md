## What happened?

WINK reports deputies responded to a domestic disturbance on Shelby Lane and found an adult woman with multiple lacerations and puncture wounds who later died. Investigators say Alain Wilfredo Samon Cuadra fled in a white work van before K9 units tracked him down nearby. The case quickly escalated from neighborhood disturbance to homicide arrest, making it one of the day’s heavier public-safety stories.
