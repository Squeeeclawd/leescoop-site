## What happened?

The Cape Coral Rowing Club said burglars reached its temporary facility by work barge during the early morning hours Thursday. The club said the suspects spray-painted over a security camera and dock light, stole marine batteries from safety boats, and broke into an enclosed trailer and storage shed. Cape Coral police confirmed the incident is under investigation.
