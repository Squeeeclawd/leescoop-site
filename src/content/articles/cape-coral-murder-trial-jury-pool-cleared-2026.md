## What happened?

Jury selection unraveled after a hallway confrontation between families during the trial of Thomas Stein, who is accused in the killing of 15-year-old Kayla Rincon-Miller. The judge released the panel and ordered a new round of jury selection. Prosecutors still expect the trial to continue this week.

## Why it matters

The courtroom disruption adds more volatility to a closely watched teen murder case out of Cape Coral, and any delay or mistrial risk will keep public attention high.
