## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers lists the farmers market for Saturday, August 29, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. in the downtown Culinary District. The listing says the market runs on Saturdays through the summer and includes produce, seafood, honey, baked goods, cheeses, meats, plants, flowers, organics, barbecue, prepared meals, crafts, pet-friendly browsing, free on-street parking, and paid garage parking.

## Who is it for?

Downtown shoppers, families, food lovers, and anyone planning a low-key Saturday morning
