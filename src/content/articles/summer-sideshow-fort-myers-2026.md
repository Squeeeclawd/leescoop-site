## What is it?

Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center is promoting Summer Sideshow for Friday, July 17 inside its Grand Atrium during Downtown Fort Myers Music Walk. The event mixes live DJs, aerial performers, flow artists, projections, vendors, food, and live painting for a more immersive circus-meets-art-party format than a standard concert night. Organizers say all ages are welcome from 6 to 9 p.m., with an 18-plus After Dark block running from 9 p.m. to midnight.

## Who is it for?

Nightlife seekers, art-forward downtown crowds, and people who want a weirder summer event than the usual bar or concert stop.
