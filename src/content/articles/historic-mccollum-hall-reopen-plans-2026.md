## What happened?

Gulf Coast News reports leaders approved a motion to move ahead with a two-story redesign for McCollum Hall that would preserve its historic facade while modernizing the inside. The plan envisions a reopened space with restaurant and community uses instead of leaving the landmark dormant. For Fort Myers, the decision matters because McCollum Hall is one of the city's most recognizable Black-history landmarks and a long-watched redevelopment site.
