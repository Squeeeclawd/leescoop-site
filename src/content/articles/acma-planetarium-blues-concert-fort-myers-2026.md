## What is it?

The Acoustic Music Association is partnering with Calusa Nature Center and Planetarium for a September 17 concert featuring blues songwriter Doug MacLeod with Michael Boswell opening. The Visit Fort Myers listing highlights MacLeod's decades-long career, award recognition, and story-driven acoustic blues. The setting gives the concert extra local texture beyond a standard stage show.

## Who is it for?

Blues fans, songwriter listeners, ACMA regulars, and anyone looking for an unusual Fort Myers concert setting
