## What happened?

The Sanibel-Captiva Conservation Foundation’s policy director, Matt DePaolis, says additional water storage is insufficient if the water cannot meet legal phosphorus limits for release into the Everglades.

His concern appears in The Florida Trib’s reporting, republished by WGCU, on the proposed **Southland mining-and-reservoir project south of Lake Okeechobee**. Project backers say it would help restoration; critics say more water-cleaning wetlands are needed. The project still needs approvals.
