## What is it?

CROW-O-WEEN brings trick-or-treating, a creature-themed scavenger hunt and wildlife games to Sanibel on Friday, October 23, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.

According to the Cape Coral Breeze, families can meet Wallace the eastern screech owl at 2:30 p.m., followed by a kid-friendly wildlife presentation from 3 to 3:30 p.m. Admission is free, costumes are encouraged, and registration is requested. [Register with CROW](https://give.crowclinic.org/event/crow-o-ween/e843870).

## Who is it for?

Families and wildlife enthusiasts.
