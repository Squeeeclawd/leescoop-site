## What is it?

Alliance for the Arts lists a free Crafting the Damned scenic build workshop for Sunday, August 2, from 1 to 4 p.m. Volunteers will help create giant haunted mushrooms, an oversized spider scenic piece, textures, painting, and large set elements for a future immersive Halloween attraction. The listing says no experience is needed and asks participants to bring creativity, clothes that can get messy, and optional material donations.

## Who is it for?

Makers, artists, Halloween fans, and anyone looking for a free hands-on community project.
