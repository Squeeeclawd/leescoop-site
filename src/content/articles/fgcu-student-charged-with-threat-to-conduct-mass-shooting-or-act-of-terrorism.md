## What happened?

WINK reports Joseph Antonio Baer, 21, was arrested after an FBI Safe Streets Task Force and Fort Myers police investigation. Court records say investigators tied him to messages praising mass-casualty attackers and discussing plans to buy a rifle after turning 21. Authorities argued the statements amounted to true threats, not protected speech.
