## What happened?

WINK reports the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission is investigating how an alligator head ended up dumped along State Road 82 in Lee County. FWC says killing an alligator outside the legal season or without proper permits is illegal. The story stands out because it is both bizarre and local, with a direct ask for tips from anyone who knows what happened.
