## What is it?

Nude Nocturne returns to Alliance for the Arts on Saturday, November 7, from 6 to 10 p.m. The lineup includes live figure drawing, body painting, music, a DJ, food trucks and cocktails. Burlesque performances require the admission-plus-burlesque ticket, with listed showtimes at 6:30, 7:30 and 8:30 p.m. Listed ticket prices range from $35 to $65 depending on membership, purchase date and package. Check the official ticket page for current availability and entry policies.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
