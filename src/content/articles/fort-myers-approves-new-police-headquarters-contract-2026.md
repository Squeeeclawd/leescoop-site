## What happened?

WINK reports Fort Myers City Council unanimously approved a $109 million construction contract for a new police headquarters after more than a decade of discussion and delays. The project is expected to include a modern 911 communications center, evidence processing, training space, an indoor firing range, and updated technology. WINK reports total project costs are expected to reach about $147 million, with construction targeted to begin in early September and last about 21 months.
