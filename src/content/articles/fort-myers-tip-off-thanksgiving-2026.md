## What is it?

The **Fort Myers Tip-Off** brings men’s and women’s college basketball to **Suncoast Credit Union Arena** during Thanksgiving week, according to Visit Fort Myers.

The tourism bureau lists these tournament dates:

- **Men’s tournament:** November 23–26, 2026.
- **Women’s tournament:** November 27–28, 2026.

The listing does not include game times or ticket prices.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
