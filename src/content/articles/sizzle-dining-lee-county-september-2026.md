## What is it?

Sizzle Dining runs September 1 through 30 at participating restaurants across Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Fort Myers Beach, Bonita Springs, and nearby communities. The promotion offers fixed-price, multi-course meals from local chefs. Each meal also generates a $1 donation to Blessings in a Backpack SWFL.

## Who is it for?

Local diners, date-night planners, restaurant explorers, and charity-minded food fans
