## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists a July 3 concert at Caloosa Sound Amphitheater as part of Downtown Fort Myers' two-night Independence Day celebration. The lineup features Listen to Her Heart, a Tom Petty tribute, and To the Limit, an Eagles tribute. General admission is free, with premium reserved tickets available, doors at 6 p.m., and the event beginning at 7 p.m.

## Who is it for?

Classic rock fans, downtown visitors, and anyone starting the July 4 weekend early.
