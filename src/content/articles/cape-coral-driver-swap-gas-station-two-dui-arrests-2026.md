## What happened?

Cape Coral Police say an anonymous caller reported a Jeep driving recklessly on Cape Coral Parkway before an officer spotted the vehicle at a 7-Eleven near Palm Tree Boulevard. Officers said one man moved into the driver seat to reposition the Jeep at the pumps before switching back, and then a second man drove away weaving into a bike lane and nearly onto a sidewalk. Police said both men showed signs of impairment, and both were arrested on DUI-related charges.
