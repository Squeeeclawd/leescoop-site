## What happened?

WINK reports Cape Coral has posted hundreds of records tied to a Florida DOGE review so residents can inspect how city money is being spent. The documents include a fiscal-year purchase-order analysis, with large infrastructure and utility-related vendors among the top spending lines. The city says it has not yet heard back from DOGE, and its official correspondence page is live for public review.
