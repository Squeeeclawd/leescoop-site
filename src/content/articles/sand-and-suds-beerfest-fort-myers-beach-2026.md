## What is it?

Sand and Suds Beerfest is scheduled for Saturday, October 10, from 1 to 5 p.m. The Visit Fort Myers listing and the Woman's Club site describe cold beer, food, local shopping and live music, with general admission and VIP ticket options. Fort Myers Beach is a separate geography and does not count toward the Fort Myers minimum.

## Who is it for?

Adults looking for a beachside food-and-drink festival
