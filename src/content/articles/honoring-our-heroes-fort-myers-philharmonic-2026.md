## What is it?

Barbara B. Mann Performing Arts Hall lists Honoring Our Heroes for Friday, September 11, at 7:30 p.m. The Fort Myers Philharmonic concert is described as an inaugural performance and a tribute to local first responders on the 25th anniversary of 9/11. The official listing places the concert at Barbara B. Mann Performing Arts Hall at FSW, with tickets starting at $65.93 including fees before tax.

## Who is it for?

Classical music audiences, families of first responders, civic groups, and residents looking for a formal remembrance event
