## What happened?

WINK News reports Oakes Farms has proposed an 'Oakes Landing' redevelopment at the former Bonita Springs dog track site on Bonita Beach Road. The plan includes a roughly 100,000-square-foot venue with bars, restaurants, a stage, pools, a playground, and other recreation space. City planning staff recommended approval while adding conditions around the oversized parking request, and the project now heads toward final City Council review.
