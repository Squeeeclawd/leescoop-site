## What is it?

Family ArtLab at Alliance for the Arts offers free, hands-on family activities on Saturday, October 17, from 10 a.m. to noon. Art-making, gallery activities, games and creative projects are part of the monthly gathering.

A free family movie follows at noon in the Foulds Theatre. Families can also explore the campus’s Morning Artisan Market, which runs from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. Check the event page for the latest details.

## Who is it for?

Families with children.
