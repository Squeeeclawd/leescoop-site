## What happened?

WINK News reports construction has begun on the Access 26 Family Food Truck Park on Estero Boulevard across from Fort Myers Beach Town Hall. Plans call for five food truck spaces, a restaurant and bar, retail shops, beach access, public restrooms, and a 45-space parking lot on a long-vacant site. Supporters frame it as another recovery project for the island, while nearby residents are still worried about traffic, noise, and over-commercialization.
