## What happened?

WINK Investigates reported that a former School District of Lee County assistant payroll director was fired after federal authorities charged her in Connecticut with health care fraud and false statements. Prosecutors allege she submitted thousands of fraudulent Medicaid claims for services never provided and collected more than $1.35 million. The district said it found no evidence she accessed district banking systems and said her employment ended on May 28 after leaders learned of the arrest.
