## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers calendar lists Palm City Cinema presents Hamilton for Friday, September 11, from 6 to 8 p.m. at Riverside Community Center. The free monthly family-friendly movie event invites guests to bring blankets, snacks, and drinks, with no alcohol or glass. The city lists the venue at 3061 E. Riverside Drive in Fort Myers.

## Who is it for?

Families, musical fans, and Fort Myers residents looking for a free neighborhood movie night
