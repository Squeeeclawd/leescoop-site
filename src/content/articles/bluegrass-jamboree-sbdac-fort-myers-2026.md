## What is it?

Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center lists Bluegrass Jamboree for Friday, August 28, from 7 to 10 p.m. The event features Florida Americana and bluegrass band Remedy Tree, with support from local jam-funk trio Caladium.

## Who is it for?

Bluegrass, Americana, live-music, and all-ages downtown-concert fans.
