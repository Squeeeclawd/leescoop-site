## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists South Pacific In Concert for July 16 at Players Circle Theater. The listing describes a two-hour concert event built around iconic Rodgers and Hammerstein songs and a sweeping wartime romance story. It is positioned as an arts-and-culture night with tickets through the theater.

## Who is it for?

Musical theater fans and date-night crowds.
