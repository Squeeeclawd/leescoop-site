## What is it?

Make a miniature holiday retreat at **Sip and Create: Thankful for Peace**, November 20 from **6–8 p.m.** at the Alliance for the Arts, **10091 McGregor Blvd., Fort Myers**.

Instructor Heidi Endress leads a tabletop Zen garden project using sand, stones, seasonal accents and miniature accessories. The Alliance says **all supplies are included**, along with two glasses of wine for participants **21 and older**.

Registration is **$55 for members** and **$66 for nonmembers**.

## Who is it for?

Adults
