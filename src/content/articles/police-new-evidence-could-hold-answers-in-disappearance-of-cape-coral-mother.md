## What happened?

WINK Investigates reports police are revisiting the disappearance of Cape Coral mother Joleen Cummings with additional witness interviews and new forensic work. Former prosecutor David LaSalandra told the outlet investigators have both existing evidence and potential newer evidence scheduled for testing. Cape Coral's police chief said the aim is to give the family a path toward peace.
