## What is it?

The 38th annual Fort Myers Beach Christmas Boat Parade is scheduled for December 5. Decorated boats travel through Matanzas Pass, with viewing opportunities along the waterfront and at bayside restaurants. Check the parade schedule before choosing a viewing spot.

Archived conceptual illustration; not a photograph of this event or its performers.

## Who is it for?

Families, boat enthusiasts and anyone who enjoys waterfront holiday lights.
