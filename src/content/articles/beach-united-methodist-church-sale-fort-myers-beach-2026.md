## What happened?

Fort Myers Beach Observer reported that Dawn and Mike Miller, the owners of The Whale, closed on the Beach United Methodist Church property on Bay Road. The building has sat vacant since Hurricane Ian and needs major restoration work, including a new roof and interior gutting. Miller said the goal is to keep the building, restore it and potentially bring in uses such as medical offices or salon space.
