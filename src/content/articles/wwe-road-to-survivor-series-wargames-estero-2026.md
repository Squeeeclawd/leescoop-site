## What is it?

Hertz Arena lists WWE Road to Survivor Series: WarGames for Thursday, November 12. Doors open at 6:30 p.m. and the live event begins at 7:30 p.m. The stop is part of a seven-date tour leading into Survivor Series: WarGames later that month.

## Who is it for?

Wrestling fans, families, and arena-event crowds looking for a major touring show
