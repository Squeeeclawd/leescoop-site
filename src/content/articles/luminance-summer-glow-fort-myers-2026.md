## What is it?

Luminance: Summer Glow is scheduled for Friday, June 12 at Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center in Downtown Fort Myers. The event starts with doors and neon body painting at 6 p.m., followed by yoga, an ecstatic dance set, and an open dance floor. The lineup also includes projection art, live DJs, local vendors, and food, with admission limited to ages 13 and up.

## Who is it for?

People looking for a visually bold night out with movement, music, and art.
