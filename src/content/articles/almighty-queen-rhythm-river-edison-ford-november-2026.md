## What is it?

Almighty Queen opens the Rhythm on the River concert season on the Ford lawn Friday, November 13, from 6:30 to 8:30 p.m. Gates open at 6 p.m. for the Queen tribute show. Bring a folding lawn chair unless you have a VIP table; food and drink vendors will be on site. Tickets are $30 for Edison Ford members and $35 for nonmembers. A $275 VIP table includes seating for up to five and early entry.
