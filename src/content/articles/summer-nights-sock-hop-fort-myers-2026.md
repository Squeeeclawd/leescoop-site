## What is it?

Alliance for the Arts lists Summer Nights Sock Hop for Saturday, Sept. 19, from 8 to 11 p.m. The event includes a DJ spinning 1950s and 1960s hits, dancing in the gallery, karaoke, a Grease sing-along in the Foulds Theatre, and encouraged retro outfits.

## Who is it for?

Families, friends, dancers, karaoke fans, and retro-music crowds.
