## What happened?

WINK reports Ladavion King appeared before a judge from a hospital bed after an I-75 standoff near Alico Road. Florida Highway Patrol charges listed by WINK include four counts of attempted second-degree murder with a firearm, discharging a firearm in public, unlawful firearm possession by a felon, and fleeing or attempting to elude law enforcement. The standoff followed a fatal shooting investigation in St. Petersburg and shut down northbound and southbound lanes for hours.
