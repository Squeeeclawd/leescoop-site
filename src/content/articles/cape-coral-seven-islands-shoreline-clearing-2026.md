## What happened?

The Cape Coral Breeze reports the Army Corps of Engineers stopped shoreline clearing tied to the Seven Islands development after red mangroves were allegedly removed along the North Spreader. A local geologist told the outlet more than 570 feet of mangroves were affected and said the work needs on-site mitigation. The developer said the contractor had permits to remove invasive species and is now coordinating next steps with agencies.
