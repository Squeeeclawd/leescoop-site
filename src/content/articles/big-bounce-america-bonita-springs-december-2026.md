## What is it?

Big Bounce America is bringing a weekend of oversized inflatables to Bonita Springs on December 5–6. The Cape Coral Breeze reports seven attractions, including a giant bounce house, a 900-foot obstacle course and a space-themed play area, plus DJs and food trucks. Sessions are separated for younger children, bigger kids and adults. Tickets start at $35; the advertised all-access ticket includes three hours on site and a timed session in the main bounce house. Hours are 9 a.m.–9 p.m. Saturday and 9 a.m.–6 p.m. Sunday at 28210 Race Track Road. Check session availability before booking.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
