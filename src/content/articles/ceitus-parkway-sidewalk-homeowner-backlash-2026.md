## What happened?

Cape Coral's planned sidewalk expansion along Ceitus Parkway is drawing complaints from residents after survey stakes appeared in front yards on the south side of the road. WINK reports the three-area project costs about $1.3 million and has homeowners worried about irrigation, landscaping, driveway changes, and restoration costs. The city says the sidewalks will close gaps and improve pedestrian safety. The disagreement turns a routine infrastructure job into a neighborhood fight over property impacts and accessibility.
