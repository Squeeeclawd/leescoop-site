## What is it?

Cape Coral’s Veterans Day Parade is scheduled for Saturday, November 7, with an 11 a.m. start. The city says the route runs west along the south side of Cape Coral Parkway from Cape Coral Street to Chester Street, honoring veterans and active service members. Check the city page for participation information and updates.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
