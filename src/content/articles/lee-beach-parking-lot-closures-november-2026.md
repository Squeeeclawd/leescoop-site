## What happened?

Four Lee County beach parking lots remain closed for restoration, but their beaches are open: **Bowditch Point Park, Lynn Hall Memorial Park, Bonita Beach Park and Little Hickory Island Beach Park**.

Visit Fort Myers’ parking guide, updated October 29, says closures began September 8, 2026. Alternatives include the Summerlin Square park-and-ride for Fort Myers Beach and small, free lots at Bonita Beach Accesses 2–9. Check the official guide before heading out.
