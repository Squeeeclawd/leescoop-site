## What happened?

Recent swatting calls in Cape Coral and Charlotte County have put Southwest Florida law enforcement on high alert. Officials say the false emergency reports are designed to trigger large police and SWAT responses, and one local incident already drew a major deployment to a neighborhood. Authorities warn the hoaxes pull officers, firefighters, and ambulances away from real emergencies and could lead to serious criminal charges.
