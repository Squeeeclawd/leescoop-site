## What is it?

Broadway Palm lists The Rainbow Fish Musical for multiple June dates, with most performances built around lunch and a 1 p.m. show. The adaptation follows the well-known children’s story about sharing, kindness, and a fish whose bright scales become a lesson instead of just decoration.

## Who is it for?

Families with young kids, grandparents, summer camps, and children’s theater fans
