## What happened?

Lee County Elections says Jenna Persons-Mulicka was appointed after former supervisor Tommy Doyle retired in January. The Fort Myers attorney and state representative for District 78 is stepping into one of the county's key constitutional offices ahead of the next election cycle. The local elections office said it expects to work with her on secure, transparent, and accessible election administration.
