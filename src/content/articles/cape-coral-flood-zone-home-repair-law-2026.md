## What happened?

WINK News reports a new state law lets homeowners skip some permit requirements for projects under $7,500, but Cape Coral says the exemption only applies to homes completely outside special flood-hazard areas. City officials say roughly 60% of Cape Coral falls inside the restricted zone. That means many residents still face the usual repair process even when they are trying to make relatively small fixes before or after storm damage.
