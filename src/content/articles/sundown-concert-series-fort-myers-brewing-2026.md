## What is it?

The Sundown Concert Series runs five Saturdays at Fort Myers Brewing Company, starting September 12 with NostalJah at 6 p.m. The Visit Fort Myers listing describes live music, food trucks, a specialty beer brewed for the series, and a golden-hour outdoor setting. General admission is free, with optional VIP wristbands and posters sold separately.

## Who is it for?

Live-music fans, brewery regulars, families with chairs, dog-friendly groups, and food-truck followers
