## What happened?

WINK News reported that Cape Coral Mayor John Gunter warned residents a proposed higher homestead exemption would slash the city's taxable value and create a roughly $46.6 million annual shortfall. The mayor said public safety would be protected, leaving other services like parks, transportation, code enforcement and operations exposed to cuts or review. The debate is tied to the level of service residents expect if statewide tax changes move forward.
