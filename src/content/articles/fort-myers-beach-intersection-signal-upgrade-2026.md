## What happened?

WINK reports the town is beginning repairs at Old San Carlos Boulevard and Estero Boulevard, a key intersection damaged after Hurricane Ian. The work includes new pedestrian signals, ADA-compliant ramps, upgraded lighting, and elevated electrical equipment meant to better survive future storms. Overnight lane closures are expected as the island continues rebuilding basic infrastructure as well as visitor traffic flow.
