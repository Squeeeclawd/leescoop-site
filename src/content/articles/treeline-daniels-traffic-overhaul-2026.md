## What happened?

WINK reports Lee County has started a $3 million project at Treeline Avenue and Daniels Parkway that will reconfigure lanes to match current traffic patterns. The redesign adds a second right-turn movement, shifts one left-turn lane into a through lane, and includes ADA upgrades such as better crosswalks and ramps. It is the kind of commuter-impact story people feel immediately because the work zone begins now and runs into late 2026.
