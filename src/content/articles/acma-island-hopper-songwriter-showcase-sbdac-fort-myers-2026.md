## What is it?

Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center lists ACMA's Island Hopper Songwriter Showcase for Thursday, September 24. The showcase features Roy Schneider, Anthony Wayne, Bill Metts, Tim McGeary, and Matt Hannam in an evening built around original songs and the stories behind them. Doors open at 6:30 p.m. and the concert starts at 7:30 p.m.

## Who is it for?

Songwriter-festival fans, acoustic music listeners, and downtown Fort Myers date-night planners
