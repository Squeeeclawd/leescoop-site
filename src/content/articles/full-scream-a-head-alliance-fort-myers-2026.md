## What is it?

Alliance for the Arts lists Full Scream A Head for Friday, Oct. 30, from 7 to 10 p.m. as the most intense edition of Stage Fright Nights: Summoning the Damned. The one-night program includes a full-touch haunted-house experience, food, drinks and outdoor Halloween activities; guests may attend without entering the haunted house. The attraction is recommended for ages 13 and up and uses strobe lighting, fog, stairs, confined spaces and sudden scares.

## Who is it for?

Haunted-house fans ages 13 and up, plus guests who only want the outdoor Halloween atmosphere.
