## What is it?

Jeff Ocasio leads a ghost-sculpting workshop at the Alliance for the Arts on October 3 from 12:30–4 PM. Participants will layer masking tape with paper, wire and glass-marble eyes to build a roughly seven-inch free-standing ghost. All supplies are included, and the class starts with a short tour of nearby artist studios. Registration is $90 for members and $110 for non-members.

## Who is it for?

Artists of all skill levels; public registration is offered.
