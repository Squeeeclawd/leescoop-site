## What is it?

*Hadestown: Teen Edition* brings the Greek myth of Orpheus and Eurydice to the Alliance for the Arts, with a folk- and jazz-inspired score.

The Alliance lists five performances December 3–6:

- **December 3 and 4:** 7:30 p.m.
- **December 5:** 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m.
- **December 6:** 2 p.m.

Shows are at **10091 McGregor Blvd., Fort Myers**. Tickets are **$25 for adults** and **$15 for students and kids**.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
