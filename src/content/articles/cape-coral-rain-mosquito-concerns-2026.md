## What happened?

Lee County Mosquito Control says standing water left by recent rain could quickly turn into breeding grounds across Cape Coral. Officials said it can take only five days during rainy season for mosquitoes to go from egg to adult. The concern is especially sharp in low-lying areas such as Jaycee Park as more tropical moisture moves into Southwest Florida.
