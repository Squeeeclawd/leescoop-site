## What happened?

WINK News reports George Beemer, 23, was ordered held without bond after his first court appearance in the Ranchette Road case. According to WINK, the Lee County Sheriff's Office says Beemer confessed to shooting his mother and setting the home on fire after a medical call and a second 911 call led deputies and firefighters to the scene. Detectives testified that the medical examiner ruled the cause of death a gunshot wound and the manner of death a homicide, and Beemer faces second-degree murder, first-degree arson, and abuse-of-a-body charges.
