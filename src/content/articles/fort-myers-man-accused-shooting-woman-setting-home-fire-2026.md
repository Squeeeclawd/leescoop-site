## What happened?

WINK reports that the Lee County Sheriff's Office charged 23-year-old George Beemer with second-degree murder, first-degree arson and abuse of a dead human body. Deputies say investigators believe the victim was shot before the Ranchette Road home was intentionally set on fire. The case remains active with the sheriff's office, the medical examiner and the State Attorney's Office.
