## What happened?

WINK asked Lee County Property Appraiser Matt Caldwell what happens if Florida cuts property tax revenue harder than local governments can absorb. Caldwell said one option is raising rates on non-homesteaded properties, while local governments could also lean on other taxes and fees to close the gap. It is a high-value civic story because the fallout would not stay abstract; it could change what residents, landlords, and businesses actually pay.
