## What happened?

WINK reports the Lee County School Board was set to review a working agreement tied to Fort Myers Beach Elementary that could lower operating costs and support a future charter-school reopening. The island campus has been closed for more than a year after flooding from Hurricanes Helene and Milton. For Beach families, it is one of the clearest signs yet that the school site may have a path back instead of staying in limbo.
