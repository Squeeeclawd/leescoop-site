## What happened?

WINK News reports Brenda Ann Allen was arrested this week after investigators accused her of running a months-long embezzlement scheme at Extreme Demolition & Land Clearing. The arrest affidavit cited by WINK says detectives found 63 unauthorized electronic vendor payments totaling $212,618 in losses and fees. Allen faces organized-scheme-to-defraud and grand-theft charges, with a court appearance scheduled for August 31.
