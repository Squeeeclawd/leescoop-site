## What happened?

WINK News reports that one person died after a two-vehicle crash at Cemetery Road and Dana Road in Fort Myers. Five other people were trauma-alerted after the crash, and the Lee County Sheriff's Office said the roads were closed while traffic was rerouted onto Skates Circle. Florida Highway Patrol is handling the investigation.
