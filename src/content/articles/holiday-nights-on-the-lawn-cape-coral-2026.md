## What is it?

Cape Coral’s Holiday Nights on the Lawn is scheduled for November 27 and 28, from 4 to 8 p.m. each evening at City Hall. The city lists tree and menorah lighting, inflatables, food trucks, ice skating, a trackless train, vendors and Santa. Each evening ends with a holiday movie, with a drone show planned for Saturday night. The city page does not state admission or activity prices; check for updates before going.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
