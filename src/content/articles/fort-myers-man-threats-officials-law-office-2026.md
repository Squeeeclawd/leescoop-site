## What happened?

WINK News reports that Adam Jeffrey Vaughn, 37, was arrested after the Lee County Sheriff's Office investigated an email and online videos containing violent threats. Sheriff Carmine Marceno said the threats targeted him, the Lee County Sheriff's Office, State Attorney Amira Fox, and the Farah & Farah law firm. Investigators also reported seizing more than three pounds of synthetic marijuana, THC products, and electronic devices while executing a search warrant.
