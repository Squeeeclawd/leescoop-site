## What happened?

Gulf Coast News reports the city plans a July 4 ceremony for the new 250-foot flagpole built at Bernice Braden Park to honor America's 250th birthday. The project has drawn attention because of the flagpole's unusual scale and prominent location near the bridge gateway into Cape Coral. That makes it an easy talker even before the holiday event itself arrives.
