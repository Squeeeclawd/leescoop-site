## What is it?

Old earrings, broken chains and mismatched jewelry get a second life at Sparkle & Shine: Upcycled Jewelry Christmas Trees. Jayne Baker leads the Alliance for the Arts workshop on Saturday, November 14, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. Participants arrange jewelry and embellishments into a Christmas-tree collage. Bring old or unused jewelry you want to reuse; the organizer says all other supplies are provided. Registration is listed at $75 for members and $90 for nonmembers.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
