## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists a June 29 through July 6 America 250 program at Edison and Ford Winter Estates. The site is offering daily history presentations at 11 a.m. and 2 p.m., patriotic displays around the museum and gardens, and special July 6 programming for Mina Edison's birthday with living-history characters and behind-the-scenes tour options. The listing says Edison and Ford Winter Estates is the only Lee County stop on the America 250 Florida road trip map.

## Who is it for?

History lovers, families, museum visitors, and locals looking for a quieter Fourth-week event.
