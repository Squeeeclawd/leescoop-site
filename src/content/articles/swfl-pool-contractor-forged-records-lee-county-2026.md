## What happened?

WINK Investigates reports that newly released court documents detail how a Southwest Florida pool contractor allegedly altered and re-filed notices of commencement with expiration dates pushed to 2044. The Lee County Sheriff's Office investigation followed homeowner complaints about unauthorized filings and unfinished pool work. The contractor surrendered at the Lee County Main Jail after warrants were issued on two felony counts of uttering a forged instrument, according to WINK.
