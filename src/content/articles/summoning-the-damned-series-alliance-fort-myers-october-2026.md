## What is it?

Five Summoning the Damned nights at Alliance for the Arts:

- Oct. 22, 7–10 p.m.: Game Over!
- Oct. 23, 7–10 p.m.: Scary Stories.
- Oct. 24, 7–10 p.m.: Rise of the Undead (Zombie Night).
- Oct. 28, 7–9 p.m.: Bare Bones (Discount Night).
- Oct. 29, 7–9 p.m.: FreakArt After Dark (Community Fundraiser Night).

See our separate coverage for [Oct. 17’s preview](/rocky-horror-outdoor-haunted-preview-fort-myers-2026/), [Oct. 30’s Full Scream A Head](/full-scream-a-head-alliance-fort-myers-2026/) and [Oct. 31’s Boos & Booze](/stage-fright-nights-boos-booze-alliance-fort-myers-2026/). Check the organizer for tickets, age guidance and updates; prices are unconfirmed.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
