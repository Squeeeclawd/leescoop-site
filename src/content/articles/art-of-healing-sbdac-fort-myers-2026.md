## What is it?

Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center lists The Art of Healing: Prescriptions for the Soul for the August Art Walk opening on Aug. 7 from 6 to 10 p.m. The exhibition and wellness event includes original artwork, sound baths, guided meditation, yoga, live music, presentations, and massage therapy, with donation-based wellness activities supporting Providence Family Life Center.

## Who is it for?

Art Walk regulars, wellness-curious locals, gallery visitors, and downtown night-out planners.
