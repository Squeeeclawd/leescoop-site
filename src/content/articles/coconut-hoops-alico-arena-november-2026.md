## What is it?

**Coconut Hoops** is on the Thanksgiving-weekend calendar at **FGCU’s Alico Arena**, with a women’s college basketball tournament listed for **November 27–29, 2026**.

Visit Fort Myers lists the three-day tournament separately from the Fort Myers Tip-Off at Suncoast Credit Union Arena. The tourism listing does not include game times or ticket prices.
