## What is it?

Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center lists Introduction to Photography for Thursdays from August 27 through October 15, 6 to 8 p.m. The course starts with vision, light, and composition before moving into aperture, shutter speed, ISO, manual exposure, a Fort Myers photo walk, and a final showcase. The listing says no experience is required, any camera is welcome, and the class is taught by Fort Myers-based photographer Kevin Boller.

## Who is it for?

Beginner photographers and creative adults who want live instruction instead of another video tutorial.
