## What is it?

South Seas Resort is hosting a Saturday afternoon golf-ball drop with an only-in-Florida hook: hundreds of golf balls are scheduled to be dropped from a helicopter at 1 p.m.

If your ball lands as a hole-in-one or closest to the pin, you can win a cash prize.

## Why this one jumps off the calendar

Most weekend listings are music, markets, or brunch. This one is a helicopter, a golf target, a cash-prize scramble, and a charity tie-in all packed into a two-hour Captiva event.

Visit Fort Myers lists the event as supporting Folds of Honor, which provides scholarships to families of fallen and disabled service members.

## Quick details

- **When:** Saturday, May 23, 2026, 1-3 p.m.
- **Where:** South Seas Resort on Captiva
- **Cost:** $25 for one golf ball or $100 for five
- **Why go:** Helicopter spectacle, cash-prize energy, resort setting, and a cause people can actually feel good about
