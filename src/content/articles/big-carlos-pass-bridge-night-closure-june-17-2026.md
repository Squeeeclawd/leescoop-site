## What happened?

WGCU reports traffic on the new Big Carlos Pass Bridge will be shut down in both directions between 8 and 10 p.m. on Wednesday, June 17. The temporary closure is meant to give crews time to finish final work before the project is completed. For drivers heading between Fort Myers Beach and Lovers Key, it is a short but highly specific disruption worth planning around.
