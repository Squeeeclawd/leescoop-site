## What happened?

LeeTran’s free downtown trolleys and Fort Myers Beach tram are scheduled to return **Thursday, November 19**, WGCU reports.

The Blue Line serves downtown Fort Myers; the Gold Line connects North Fort Myers with downtown. Both run daily, 11 a.m.–11 p.m. The beach tram links the public library and Bowditch Point Park about every 30–40 minutes. Riders should check updated schedules before traveling.
