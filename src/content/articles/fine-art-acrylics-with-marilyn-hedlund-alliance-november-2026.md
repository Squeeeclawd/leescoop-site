## What is it?

Artist Marilyn Hedlund opens and closes the session with a group critique. It gives painters a chance to hear how others read their work as well as discuss technique. The Alliance describes the class as suitable for artists at any level; check whether the November 25 session accepts new participants.

[Official event details](https://www.artinlee.org/event/fine-art-acrylics-with-marilyn-hedlund-7/2026-11-25/).
