## What happened?

WINK News reported that Lee Health has started work on a 100,000-square-foot ambulatory clinic off Veterans Memorial Parkway next to Lowe's in Cape Coral. Leaders said the site will bring oncology, cardiology, orthopedic, and imaging services to a rapidly growing part of the city while easing pressure on Cape Coral Hospital. Lee Health also said the project could add roughly 100 to 150 jobs and is expected to finish in fall 2027.
