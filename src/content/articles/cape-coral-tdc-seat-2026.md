## What happened?

The Cape Coral Breeze reports a local bill signed by Gov. Ron DeSantis will expand the Lee County Tourist Development Council from nine seats to 11 and reserve a standing voting seat for Cape Coral as the county's largest municipality. The measure also changes how two voting seats are selected. The shift gives Cape Coral a more direct voice in how local tourism priorities and dollars are shaped.
