## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists Magical Mystery Doors for June 7 at Arts Bonita in Bonita Springs. The show pitches a mashup-heavy tribute format built around music from The Beatles, Led Zeppelin, and The Doors, with a separate Zeppelin-focused segment also described in the listing. For readers scanning for a recognizable live-music night beyond the usual bar calendar, this is one of the more obvious talkers.
