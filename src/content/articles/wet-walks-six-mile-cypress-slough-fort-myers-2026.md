## What is it?

Friends of Six Mile Cypress Slough Preserve lists Wet Walks 2026 as a multi-date guided series at the Fort Myers preserve. The walks take participants into water-filled restricted areas that are normally off-limits, with expert guides and age limits for safety. The season runs through Oct. 31, with regular and themed walks listed by the organizer.

## Who is it for?

Nature lovers age 12 and older who are comfortable walking through wetland water
