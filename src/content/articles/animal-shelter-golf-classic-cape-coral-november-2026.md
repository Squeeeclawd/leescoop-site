## What is it?

The Cape Coral Animal Shelter Golf Classic returns to Palmetto-Pine Country Club on Saturday, November 7. The Cape Coral Breeze reports a 7 a.m. tee-off and a 2 p.m. finish.

The shelter fundraiser includes golf, on-course contests and raffles, with proceeds supporting animal care. Check [organizer registration and sponsorship information](https://capecoralanimalshelter.com/golf-classic) for current pricing and availability before making plans.

## Who is it for?

Golfers and people supporting local shelter animals.
