## What is it?

Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center opens its annual Dark Art exhibition during Art Walk on October 2. The show focuses on mysterious, macabre, surreal, gothic, eerie, and unconventional visual art, with costumes encouraged on opening night. The reception runs from 6 to 10 p.m., and the exhibition remains open through October 15.

## Who is it for?

Art Walk visitors, Halloween-season crowds, artists, and anyone drawn to strange or gothic work
