## What is it?

Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center lists its inaugural Education Open House Party for Friday, Aug. 21 from 5 to 8 p.m. The free, family-friendly evening brings together arts, culture, music, education, STEM, advocacy, and library partners under one roof. The listing says visitors can expect hands-on workshops, live demonstrations, performances, showcases, a kids dance party, raffle baskets, a food truck, and rooftop lounge access.

## Who is it for?

Families, students, educators, and downtown Music Walk visitors.
