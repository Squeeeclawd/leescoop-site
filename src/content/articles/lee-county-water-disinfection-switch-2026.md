## What happened?

WGCU reported that Lee County Utilities will switch from a free chlorine residual back to chloramines as the primary disinfectant on May 21. The change has practical consequences for residents using home dialysis equipment, aquariums, and other sensitive systems.

This is a local update worth knowing for residents, visitors, or nearby businesses.
