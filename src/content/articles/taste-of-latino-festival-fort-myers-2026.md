## What is it?

The free Taste of Latino Festival returns to Centennial Park on September 5 to kick off Hispanic Heritage Month. Organizers list more than 30 food, cultural, and business vendors along with the Top Latino Chef contest and an Empanada Smackdown. The event runs from noon to 5 p.m. on the downtown riverfront.

## Who is it for?

Families, food fans, downtown visitors, and anyone celebrating Southwest Florida's Latino cultures
