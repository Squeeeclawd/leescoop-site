## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers lists Monthly Art Walk for Friday, August 7, from 5 to 9 p.m. The River District Alliance event lines downtown streets with local and regional artists selling and displaying one-of-a-kind work, with multiple styles represented. The city listing says the event is free to attend.

## Who is it for?

Art buyers, downtown walkers, date-night planners, and anyone looking for a free evening out
