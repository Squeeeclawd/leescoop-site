## What is it?

Players Circle Theater lists Into the Woods for July 30 through August 2, with opening night on Thursday, July 30, from 7:30 to 10 p.m. The theater describes the Sondheim musical as a collision of favorite fairy tales about what happens after wishes come true. Tickets are listed at $20 to $30.

## Who is it for?

Musical-theater fans, Sondheim people, and families with older kids who like big fairy-tale stories
