## What is it?

Broadway Palm describes The Sounds of 1967 as a musical trip through the big scenes of that year — London mod, Detroit Motown, Los Angeles folk-pop, and San Francisco psychedelia. The show runs May 22 through June 20 with lunch, dinner, matinee, evening, and twilight options depending on the date.

## Who is it for?

Classic-hits fans, dinner-theater regulars, date-night couples, and visitors looking for an easy Fort Myers show
