## What happened?

WINK reports a fresh controversy at Cape Coral's Seven Islands site after nearby residents saw a long stretch of mangroves cleared along the waterfront. The station says the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers told it no mangrove removal had been approved as part of the project's ongoing permit review. Neighbors are now pressing for answers about how the clearing happened and whether the shoreline damage can be repaired.
