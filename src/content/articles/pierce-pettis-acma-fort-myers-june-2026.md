## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists ACMA Presents Pierce Pettis in Concert for June 20, with doors at 6:30 p.m. and the show starting at 7 p.m. Pettis is billed as a veteran songwriter with deep roots in folk, country, and Americana, and Pete Benson is set to open. It is a smaller-room pick, but a strong one for readers who care about serious live music rather than giant arena scale.

## Who is it for?

Songwriter fans and Americana listeners
