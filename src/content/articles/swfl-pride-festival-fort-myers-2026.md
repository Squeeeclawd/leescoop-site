## What is it?

SWFL Pride Festival is scheduled for Saturday, June 13 in the River District from 2 to 10 p.m. The event listing says the free street festival will feature live entertainment, local vendors, food, music, and community programming. A rooftop dance party at Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center and VIP ticket options are also listed.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a big downtown community festival with music and nightlife.
