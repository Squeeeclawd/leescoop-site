## What happened?

WINK News reports that Jesse William Korff, 32, was sentenced to 24 years in federal prison after pleading guilty to firearms and explosives offenses. Authorities said the case began with a Fort Myers police response to a 911 hang-up call that led officers to a firearm, silencer and pipe bomb. WINK reports the Fort Myers Police Department, Lee County Sheriff's Office and ATF investigated the case.
