## What is it?

Alliance Morning Market returns June 20 at Alliance for the Arts in Fort Myers. Organizers describe it as a family-friendly artisan market with vendors, live entertainment, free family art programming and a free movie on campus. Admission is free, with the event designed to turn the arts campus into a longer Saturday community hangout.

## Who is it for?

Families, local shoppers and arts-minded weekend crowds
