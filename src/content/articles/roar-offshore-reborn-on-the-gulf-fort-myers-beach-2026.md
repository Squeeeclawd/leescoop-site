## What is it?

The Fort Myers Beach Chamber calendar lists ROAR Offshore's Reborn on the Gulf weekend for September 11 through 13. The event brings premier offshore racing teams to the Gulf shoreline, with boats on display, racing, entertainment, and a comeback theme tied to Fort Myers Beach's recovery after Hurricane Ian. The listing points readers to ROAR Offshore for current schedule and viewing details.

## Who is it for?

Powerboat fans, beach visitors, families, and anyone following Fort Myers Beach's comeback events
