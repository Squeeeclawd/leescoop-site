## What happened?

Lee County commissioners approved a $3.9 million contract to restore Bowditch Point Park, a Fort Myers Beach destination that has stayed battered and partly closed since Hurricane Ian. WINK reports the vote finally puts real money behind repairs after years of fencing, storm damage, and stalled expectations. This has broad reader value because it touches beach recovery, public access, and whether one of the area's best-known shoreline parks can fully come back.
