## What happened?

WINK News reports the Lee Board of County Commissioners approved nearly $6.5 million for 13 beach and shoreline projects. The money is tied to Tourist Development Tax revenue and is meant to support public access, boardwalks, restrooms, and related beach facilities. The broader 2025-2026 shoreline program budget is reported at more than $29 million.
