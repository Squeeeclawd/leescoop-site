## What is it?

The Lee County Black History Society is hosting the 2026 Juneteenth Celebration on June 20 at the Fort Myers Stars Complex. The event is billed as a free, all-ages gathering focused on freedom, community and cultural heritage. Organizers say the evening will include live music, food, storytelling and a fireworks finale.

## Who is it for?

Families, community groups and anyone looking for a major local Juneteenth event
