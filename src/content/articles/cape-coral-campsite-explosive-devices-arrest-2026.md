## What happened?

WINK News reports deputies found a wooded campsite near NE 24th Avenue and Diplomat Parkway East while searching for a wanted person. Investigators say the Lee County Sheriff's Office Bomb Squad recovered seven suspected devices, including modified fireworks and a pipe-style device packed with energetic powder. Authorities say the 21-year-old suspect admitted building the devices and adding paper clips, pellets, and BBs meant to fragment on detonation.
