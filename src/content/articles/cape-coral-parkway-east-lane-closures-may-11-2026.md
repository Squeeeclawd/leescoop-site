## What happened?

Cape Coral says lane closures will begin the week of Monday, May 11, on Cape Coral Parkway East as part of the city's six-lane improvement project. The city says through traffic will remain open with at least two lanes in each direction and no detours are planned. Closures are expected during off-peak daytime hours.
