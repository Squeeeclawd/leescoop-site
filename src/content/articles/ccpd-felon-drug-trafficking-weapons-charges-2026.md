## What happened?

Cape Coral police say narcotics detectives arrested Johnny Glover during a June 24 buy-bust operation in the parking lot of a Circle K on Southeast 26th Street. Investigators say he arrived in a rideshare vehicle and was carrying fentanyl, cocaine, other illegal narcotics, a handgun, and a pink taser despite being a convicted felon. Detectives also tied a second suspect already in custody to prior drug transactions in the same investigation. Glover was booked on multiple possession, firearm, paraphernalia, and meth trafficking charges.
