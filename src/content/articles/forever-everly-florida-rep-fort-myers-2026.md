## What is it?

Florida Repertory Theatre lists Forever Everly: The Everly Brothers Rockumentary beginning Sept. 15 at 7 p.m. The production blends live music and film, with performers Jared Mancuso and Nick Gallardo playing the duo’s songs while the story behind the music unfolds.

## Who is it for?

Theatergoers, classic pop fans, date-night planners, and River District arts regulars.
