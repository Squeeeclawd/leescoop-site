## What happened?

Gulf Coast News reports Lee County deputies stopped a vehicle on US-41 near Lazy Days Village and say the encounter escalated when detectives spotted a bag of suspected cocaine as a passenger got out. Investigators said the search turned up more than 44 grams of cocaine plus paraphernalia in a shoebox linked to the passenger. The woman was arrested on trafficking and possession charges, making it a higher-stakes local drug case rather than a minor traffic stop.
