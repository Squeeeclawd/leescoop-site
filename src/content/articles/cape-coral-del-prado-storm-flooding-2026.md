## What happened?

WINK News reported that a quick but intense thunderstorm left localized flooding in Cape Coral near Del Prado Boulevard south of Veterans. One resident said his rain gauge showed roughly five to seven inches in a short span, and he lost power about 10 times. The scene is a sharp early reminder of how quickly rainy-season street flooding can hit Cape neighborhoods.
