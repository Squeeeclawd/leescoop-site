## What happened?

WGCU reported that Fort Myers Beach’s planning agency voted against a redevelopment proposal for the former Outrigger Beach Resort site. The fight blends redevelopment pressure, coastal recovery politics, and land-use conflict in one of the county’s most sensitive post-Ian areas.
