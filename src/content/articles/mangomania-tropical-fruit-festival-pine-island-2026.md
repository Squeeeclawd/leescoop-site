## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists MangoMania for July 11 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. in St. James City. The event promises tropical plants, fruit and tree sales, recipe contests, kids games, live music, food, planting seminars, and island businesses. It is the kind of weirdly specific Florida festival that has real shareable pull.

## Who is it for?

Families, food lovers, and island visitors
