## What is it?

The South Seas Captiva Island Triathlon starts September 27 with a 7:15 a.m. super-sprint wave. Race options include sprint triathlon, duathlon, aquabike, relay, and a shorter beginner-friendly super sprint. The course combines a Gulf swim, roads closed to vehicle traffic, and a run through the island resort.

## Who is it for?

Triathletes, first-time multisport racers, spectators, and families planning an island race weekend
