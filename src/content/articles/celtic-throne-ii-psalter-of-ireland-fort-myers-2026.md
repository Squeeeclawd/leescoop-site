## What is it?

Barbara B. Mann is promoting Celtic Throne II — Psalter of Ireland for June 30 at 7:30 p.m. The show blends Irish dance, live music, cinematic visuals, and large-scale stage design. The production is pitched as a high-energy touring spectacle rather than a small recital-style stop.

## Who is it for?

Families and live performance fans
