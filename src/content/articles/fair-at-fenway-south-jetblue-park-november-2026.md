## What is it?

The Fair at Fenway South returns to JetBlue Park on November 6–8, 13–15 and 20–22. Rides, midway games and fair food share the grounds with shows included in admission. The first weekend’s lineup includes magic, circus thrills, a petting zoo and Dinosaur Takeover; racing pigs join for weekends two and three. Hours are 5–11 p.m. Fridays, 3–11 p.m. Saturdays and 3–10 p.m. Sundays. A one-day October 23 flash sale advertises $30 combo passes covering admission, unlimited rides and shows. Advance admission is listed at $10, with parking another $10 per vehicle. Check the fair’s current ticket terms before buying.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
