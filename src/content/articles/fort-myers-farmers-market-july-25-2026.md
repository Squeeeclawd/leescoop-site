## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers calendar lists the Fort Myers Farmers Market for Saturday, July 25, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. The market runs along First Street to Broadway and Broadway to Main Street with local produce, seafood, honey, baked goods, plants, prepared meals, crafts, and pet-friendly browsing.

## Who is it for?

Weekend shoppers, food lovers, downtown residents, and families.
