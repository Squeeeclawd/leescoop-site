## What is it?

The City of Sanibel Recreation Department invites families, residents and visitors to Sanibel Community Park on November 28 for Sanibel Shines: A Tree Lighting Tradition. The gathering starts at 5:30 p.m.; the city announcement does not give a separate time for switching on the lights.

Residents, families and visitors
