## What is it?

Sugarshack Downtown is hosting the second annual Downtown Showdown, a live singing competition backed by the Sugarshack All-Stars Band. Ten semifinalists compete August 11 for five finale spots, followed by the August 18 grand finale and a $2,500 top prize.

## Who is it for?

Live-music fans, supporters of local singers, and groups looking for a free Tuesday night out
