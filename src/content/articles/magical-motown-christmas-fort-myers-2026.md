## What is it?

A Magical Motown Christmas is scheduled at Barbara B. Mann Performing Arts Hall on November 27 at 7:30 p.m. The holiday-themed tribute show draws on music associated with the Temptations, Jackson Five, Diana Ross and the Supremes, Marvin Gaye, Stevie Wonder and Smokey Robinson. Expect vocal harmonies, choreography and seasonal staging—not appearances by those original artists. Check the venue’s official ticket link for current prices and availability.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
