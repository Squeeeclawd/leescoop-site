## What is it?

Beach Talk Radio reports Town Council approved the 2026 JobuFest event for May 30. With work still happening at Sandy Bottom, this year’s version is shifting to The Whale from 11 a.m. to 6:15 p.m. The event is framed as a beachy pre-hurricane-season ritual with obvious local personality.

## Who is it for?

Beach locals and weekend visitors
