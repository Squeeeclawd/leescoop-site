## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists the Full Moon Wine & Waves Tasting Cruise for June 3 aboard the Silver King at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village. The listing says guests board at 7:15 p.m., depart at 7:30 p.m., and return around 9 p.m. after a sunset-and-moonrise cruise with wine, fruit, cheese, and snacks. Limited seating and a waterfront evening format make it one of the more distinctive early-June date-night style events on the local calendar.

## Who is it for?

Couples, visitors, and locals looking for a waterfront date-night style outing
