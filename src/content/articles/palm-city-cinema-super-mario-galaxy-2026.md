## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers lists Palm City Cinema presents Super Mario Galaxy for Friday, August 14, from 6 to 8 p.m. at Riverside Community Center. The listing says the free family-friendly movie starts at 6 p.m. and guests may bring blankets, snacks, and drinks, but no alcohol or glass.

## Who is it for?

Families, kids, movie-night people, and anyone with Mario nostalgia
