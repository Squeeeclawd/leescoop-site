## What happened?

Cape Coral police say officers responded to a reckless-driving call around 11:28 p.m. Tuesday and tracked down a silver Dodge Ram after it was seen speeding through parking lots and ignoring stop signs. Investigators say the driver bailed out during a traffic stop, ran on foot, and was found hiding beneath machinery inside a car wash. Police reported signs of impairment, a suspended license, and multiple liquor bottles in the truck. The arrest ended with DUI, refusal, license, resisting, and careless-driving charges.
