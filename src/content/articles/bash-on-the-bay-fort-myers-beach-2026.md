## What is it?

Bash on the Bay takes over the Fort Myers Beach waterfront from September 5 through 7 for three days of live music, food, vendors, boating activities, contests, a beer garden, a kids zone, and a cornhole tournament. The official page places the Labor Day weekend festival at On the Bay Restaurant and Petey's Upper Deck on Crescent Street.

## Who is it for?

Beachgoers, families, boaters, live-music fans, and groups looking for a full Labor Day weekend outing
