## What is it?

A.J. Croce brings Croce Plays Croce to Barbara B. Mann Performing Arts Hall on Sunday, November 29, at 8 p.m. The concert combines Jim Croce songs such as “Operator,” “Time in a Bottle” and “Bad, Bad Leroy Brown” with A.J.’s own material and stories. The venue lists tickets starting at $51.48, including processing fees and sales tax. Lobby doors open an hour before the performance, with theatre doors opening 30 minutes before showtime.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
