## What is it?

Edison and Ford Winter Estates lists thousands of lights and hundreds of historic decorations for its 51st Annual Holiday Nights Celebration. The first stretch runs November 27 through December 23. December 24 and 25 are not on the schedule; the lights return December 26 through January 3. Check the estates’ evening admission information rather than relying on daytime museum hours.
