## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers lists the August Monthly Music Walk for Friday, August 21, from 6 to 10 p.m. Downtown streets host local and regional musicians, with lineups varying month to month. The city listing says Music Walk is free to attend.

## Who is it for?

Live-music fans, downtown regulars, families, and anyone looking for a free Friday night
