## What is it?

Slipaway Food Truck Park & Marina is selling a July 4 bash from 4 to 11 p.m. along the Caloosahatchee waterfront in Cape Coral. The event pitches a more premium holiday setup with live tribute music, shade structures, cooling misters, private restrooms, food and drinks, and prime views of the city's Red, White and Boom fireworks. General admission is limited and the venue warns that surrounding road closures will change parking and drop-off plans.

## Who is it for?

Adults, groups, and waterfront fireworks watchers who want a more controlled Cape Coral holiday-night setup.
