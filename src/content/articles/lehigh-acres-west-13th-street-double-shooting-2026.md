## What happened?

Gulf Coast News reports the Lee County Sheriff's Office is investigating a shooting in the 200 block of West 13th Street in Lehigh Acres where two people died. Neighbors told the station the area is usually quiet and that they did not recognize the people involved. Deputies called the case fluid and said more information would be released as the investigation develops.
