## What is it?

The Lee County Library System lists Art Lab: Animal Fun for Tuesday, Sept. 22, from 4 to 5 p.m. at South County Regional Library. The free hands-on program is designed for children ages 6 to 11, with registration required and space capped at 20 children. The library's practical advice is simple: dress for mess.

## Who is it for?

Children ages 6-11 and their caregivers.
