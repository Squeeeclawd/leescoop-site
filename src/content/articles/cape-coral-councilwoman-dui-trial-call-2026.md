## What happened?

WINK reports that Cape Coral City Councilwoman Rachel Kaduk was expected in court Wednesday for a trial call in her DUI case. She was arrested in December and faces DUI and breath-test refusal charges. WINK says Kaduk has pleaded not guilty and completed DUI school. The case carries extra public-interest weight because it involves a sitting elected official.
