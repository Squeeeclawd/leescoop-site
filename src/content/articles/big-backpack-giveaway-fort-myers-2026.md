## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers calendar lists the 27th Annual Big Backpack Giveaway for Sunday, July 26, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. at the Skatium. The listing describes a curbside pickup event with backpacks, school supplies, other giveaways, live music, and entertainers along the carline. Eligible children ages 5 to 12 must be in the car to receive backpacks.

## Who is it for?

Families with school-age children, especially ages 5 to 12
