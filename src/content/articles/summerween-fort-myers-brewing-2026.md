## What is it?

Fort Myers Brewing Company's SummerWeen returns Saturday, September 5, with a Halloween-in-summer celebration built around costumes, specialty beer and seltzer releases, food trucks, and a watermelon-carving contest. Doors open at noon, the kids costume contest starts at 6:30 p.m., the adults contest follows at 6:45 p.m., and live music starts at 7 p.m. as the brewery launches its new Sundown Concert Series.

## Who is it for?

Families, costume lovers, craft-beer fans, live-music crowds, and anyone ready to start spooky season early
