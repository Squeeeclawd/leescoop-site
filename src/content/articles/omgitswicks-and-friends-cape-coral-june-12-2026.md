## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists omgitswicks and Friends for June 12 at Cultural Park Theatre in Cape Coral, with performances at 6 p.m. and 8:30 p.m. The listing says the set leans into Florida-specific comedy and includes Ken Miller and Myke Herlihy on the bill. It is a stronger entertainment pick than a generic calendar filler because the act already carries a built-in online audience and a local-insider hook.
