## What is it?

Alliance for the Arts presents Sprinkles: Decoded on Friday, September 11, from 7 to 8:30 p.m. The free Cinema for Change program combines an interactive film with audience participation and a discussion led by director Andrew Korzenik. The 9/11 commemoration focuses on how people consume, interpret, and respond to information in a complex media landscape.

## Who is it for?

Adults and older students interested in film, media literacy, civic discussion, and community reflection
