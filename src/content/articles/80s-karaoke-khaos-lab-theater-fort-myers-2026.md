## What is it?

The Laboratory Theater of Florida is turning 1980s karaoke into a theatrical fundraiser. Guests can donate $5 to sing, pay $10 to nominate a friend, or trigger a reverse card that sends the original nominator to the microphone instead.

## Who is it for?

Karaoke fans, theater supporters, friend groups, and anyone willing to sing or nominate somebody else
