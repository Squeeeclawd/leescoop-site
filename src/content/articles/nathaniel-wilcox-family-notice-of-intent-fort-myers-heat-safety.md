## What happened?

WINK reports the family of 22-year-old Nathaniel Wilcox sent a notice of intent to the City of Fort Myers, the Fort Myers Fire Department and the Florida Department of Financial Services. Under Florida law, that notice is a required pre-lawsuit step against a government entity, and no lawsuit has been filed yet. The family says it also wants policy changes that could help prevent another heat-related training death.
