## What happened?

Cape Coral Police say officers responded to a disturbance call at a home on Diplomat Parkway West shortly after midnight on June 12 and found a large party inside. Investigators said many attendees appeared to be juveniles, with alcoholic drinks and marijuana visible, and several teens said the event had been promoted on social media. Police arrested the resident on an open house party charge after concluding he allowed the gathering and failed to stop underage drinking and drug use.
