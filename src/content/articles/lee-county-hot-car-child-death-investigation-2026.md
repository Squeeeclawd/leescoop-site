## What happened?

WINK reports Lee County deputies responded Tuesday evening to the 7800 block of College Parkway after a 17-month-old girl was found dead inside a hot car. The sheriff's office says the death investigation remains active and deputies are working with the State Attorney's Office. Gulf Coast News also reported LCSO's confirmation of the location, age, and ongoing investigation.
