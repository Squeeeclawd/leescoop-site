## What is it?

Jim Lauderdale brings his catalog of Americana, country, and roots music to Arts Bonita for a solo concert. The Grammy-winning singer-songwriter is known both for his own recordings and for songs recorded by artists including George Strait, Patty Loveless, The Chicks, and Blake Shelton.

## Who is it for?

Americana and country fans, songwriter enthusiasts, and listeners who appreciate an intimate solo concert
