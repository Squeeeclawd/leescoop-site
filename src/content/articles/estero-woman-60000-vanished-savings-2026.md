## What happened?

WINK reports Dolores Dagrosa says two wire transfers sent to someone she does not know drained more than $60,000 from her accounts. She told the outlet she reported the loss within hours and then filed complaints with Citibank, the Lee County Sheriff's Office, the FBI, the FTC, the Florida Attorney General's Office, and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. Dagrosa said the missing money disrupted travel plans and left her worried about basic bills while she waits for answers.
