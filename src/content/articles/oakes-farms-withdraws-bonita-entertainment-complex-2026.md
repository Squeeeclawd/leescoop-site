## What happened?

Oakes Farms withdrew its application to turn the old Bonita Springs greyhound track into an entertainment complex called Oakes Landing. Residents had split over the plan, with some backing the destination concept and others warning about traffic and noise. City staff received the formal withdrawal request earlier this week, and the mayor said the proposal had not yet reached zoning or council review.
