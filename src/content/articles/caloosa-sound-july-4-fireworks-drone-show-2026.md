## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists the July 4 celebration at Caloosa Sound Amphitheater from 6 p.m. to 10:30 p.m. The downtown event includes live music, fireworks, a drone show, rooftop experiences, food and beverage concessions, and family-friendly Fourth of July programming. General admission for the lawn is free, while premium tickets include theater seating, a private bar, and air-conditioned restrooms.

## Who is it for?

Families, downtown crowds, fireworks watchers, and visitors looking for a bigger Fort Myers holiday plan.
