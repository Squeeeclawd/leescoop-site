## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists Freedom & Fireworks at Rooftop at Riverside as a July 4 night party tied to Bonita Springs' 25th annual Independence Day tradition. The event runs from 5 p.m. until close with DJ entertainment, second-floor access, patriotic party atmosphere, and VIP table packages with food and drinks. The listing says a portion of proceeds supports Friends of BERT.

## Who is it for?

Adults and groups looking for a more elevated Bonita Springs fireworks-night plan.
