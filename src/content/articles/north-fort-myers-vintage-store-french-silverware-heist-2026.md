## What happened?

WINK News reported that two people entered a North Fort Myers antique store as customers and allegedly left with rare Parisian silverware worth about $10,000. The case stands out because the stolen items were described as unusual, high-value pieces rather than generic retail merchandise. It is the kind of brazen local theft that will get attention fast among small-business owners and shoppers.
