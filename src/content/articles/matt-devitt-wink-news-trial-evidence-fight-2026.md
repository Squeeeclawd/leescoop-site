## What happened?

Fort Myers News-Press reports that Matt Devitt and WINK News are headed toward a September 2027 jury trial in Lee County. The article says recent filings show WINK attorneys seeking casino records and other third-party documents, while Devitt's legal team argues the requests are overbroad, irrelevant, and unduly burdensome. The dispute stems from the station's ongoing lawsuit against its former chief meteorologist.
