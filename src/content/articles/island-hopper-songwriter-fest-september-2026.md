## What is it?

The annual Island Hopper Songwriter Fest is scheduled for Sept. 18 through Sept. 27. Organizers say the event will spread across Southwest Florida beach and island venues over 10 days. It remains one of the area's most recognizable music-festival style draws.

## Who is it for?

Live music fans and visitors
