## What is it?

Irving Berlin’s White Christmas runs November 20–December 30 at Broadway Palm. The musical follows performers staging a Christmas show to help their former general’s Vermont inn. Opening night has dinner at 5:30 p.m. and curtain at 7:30 p.m.; later matinee, evening and twilight times vary. Base prices are $70 show-only, $80 lunch-and-show and $90 dinner-and-show, plus a $10 White Christmas surcharge per ticket and a $3 nonrefundable ticket fee.
