## What happened?

County representatives advanced the proposal to combine the Iona-McGregor and Fort Myers Beach fire districts. Critics argue there is still no completed impact report and say the merger could weaken firefighter-paramedic coverage on the island. Supporters want voters to decide the issue next year.

## Why it matters

If critics are right, the merger could change how quickly specialized fire and rescue crews respond on the island, making this more than just another government reshuffle.
