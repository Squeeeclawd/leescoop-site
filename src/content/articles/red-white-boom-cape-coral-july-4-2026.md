## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists Red, White & BOOM for July 4 from 5 p.m. to 10 p.m. at the foot of the Cape Coral Bridge. The city describes it as the largest single-day event in Southwest Florida, built around fireworks, live entertainment, and a big family crowd. It is a classic headline slot because the scale is already baked in.

## Who is it for?

Families and holiday crowds
