## What happened?

WINK Investigations reports that downtown Fort Myers and surrounding areas could see roughly $8 billion to $10 billion in capital investment over the next decade. The story cites city redevelopment space, a new Lee Health medical campus, City of Palms possibilities, yacht basin work, high-rise residential projects, a future police station, and new office space. Residents quoted by WINK raised concerns about traffic, infrastructure, waterfront views, and neighborhood character.
