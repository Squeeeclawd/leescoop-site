## What happened?

WINK Investigates reports that more than 41 percent of Fort Myers Beach hotel rooms that existed before Hurricane Ian are still closed. The outlet says 985 of 2,380 pre-storm rooms remain dark even as countywide lodging has reopened above 86 percent. The split is especially stark between large resorts with insurance capital and smaller historic motels that still cannot finance a comeback.
