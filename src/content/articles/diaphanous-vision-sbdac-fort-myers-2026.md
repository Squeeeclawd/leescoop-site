## What is it?

Diaphanous Vision opens September 4 during the downtown Art Walk window at Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center. The exhibit by Beth Carter Bell explores life, ecology, and reconciliation with the natural world through a contemporary art framework. Gallery admission is by donation, with regular weekday gallery hours and extended Friday hours when available.

## Who is it for?

Art Walk regulars, gallery visitors, environmental art fans, and downtown date-night planners
