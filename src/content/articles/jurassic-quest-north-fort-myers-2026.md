## What is it?

Jurassic Quest returns for three days with life-size moving dinosaurs, fossil digs, rides, inflatables, and hands-on attractions. The touring family show takes over the Lee County Civic Center complex at Mike Greenwell Regional Park, with a sensory- and mobility-friendly session scheduled Saturday from 8 to 9 a.m.

## Who is it for?

Families, dinosaur fans, young explorers, and anyone looking for a big indoor weekend activity
