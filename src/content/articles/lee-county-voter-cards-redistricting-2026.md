## What happened?

The Lee County Supervisor of Elections has started mailing updated voter information cards tied to the new congressional map. Gulf Coast News reports roughly 109,000 voters are affected by district changes and about 45,000 more will get cards because of polling place name or location changes. This matters because a lot of people who think nothing changed may now have a different district or voting site before the primary.

This is a local update worth knowing for residents, visitors, or nearby businesses.
