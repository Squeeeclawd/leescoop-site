## What happened?

Residents gathered enough signatures to push recall efforts against councilmembers John King and Karen Woodson. The fight centers on allegations, redevelopment tensions, and a lawsuit aimed at stopping the recall. Election officials say the vote could cost taxpayers about $20,000 if it moves forward.

## Why it matters

This fight goes straight at who controls Fort Myers Beach's redevelopment future, and the political fallout could shape major land-use decisions while also costing taxpayers money.
