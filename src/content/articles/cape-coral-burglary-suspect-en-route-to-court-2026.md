## What happened?

Cape Coral police say officers responded to an occupied RV after a man repeatedly tried to open its locked door and then disconnected the vehicle from power. Investigators identified the suspect as Devon Turner and said he told officers he was on his way to court and searching for clothing. Police determined he was trying to unlawfully enter the occupied dwelling with intent to steal, and they also reported finding methamphetamine in his backpack during the arrest. The story has obvious talk value because the alleged burglary happened while the suspect was reportedly headed to court for another burglary case.
