## What is it?

Wine and Canvas is hosting a Fall Day on the Farm painting session at Whispering Oaks Farm on August 22. The ticket includes painting supplies, guided instruction, goat feed, and an animal encounter during the first 15 to 20 minutes. The private farm requires visitors to preregister before entry.

## Who is it for?

Casual painters, animal lovers, families and friends looking for an unusual Saturday workshop
