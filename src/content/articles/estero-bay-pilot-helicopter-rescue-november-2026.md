## What happened?

A sheriff’s helicopter rescued a pilot after his floatplane made an emergency landing in **Estero Bay Preserve on Sunday, November 8**, according to the Cape Coral Breeze’s November 9 report.

The remote spot could not be reached by land, and the water was too shallow for a boat rescue. The crew lowered a basket and lifted the pilot to safety. He was not believed to be seriously injured. The FAA is investigating.
