## What is it?

Cape Coral says its annual Hurricane Expo will run Saturday, May 30, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. at the German American Club. More than 30 organizations are expected to share preparedness resources, evacuation guidance, and supply-kit advice. The free event is designed to help residents prepare before hurricane season ramps up.

## Who is it for?

Residents and families
