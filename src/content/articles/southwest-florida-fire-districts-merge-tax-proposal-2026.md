## What happened?

WINK reports state Rep. Mike Giallombardo says some independent fire districts in Southwest Florida may eventually have to merge if the proposed homestead-tax change wins voter approval. Fire chiefs have warned that special districts could lose millions because they rely heavily on property-tax revenue for stations, staffing and equipment. Bonita Springs Fire's chief told WINK his department alone could lose roughly $8 million over two years.
