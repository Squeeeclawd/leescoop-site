## What happened?

WINK News reports that Cape Coral police officer Travis Harrison was arrested after an off-duty disturbance in Lee County. According to WINK, the Lee County Sheriff's Office and the State Attorney's Office are handling the criminal investigation, while the Cape Coral Police Department opened a separate internal affairs review. The department said Harrison was placed on administrative leave and is presumed innocent unless proven guilty.
