## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists Tropical Summer Party at Rooftop for Friday, June 26, from 7 to 10 p.m. at Rooftop at Riverside in Bonita Springs. The night features live music by Roots Almighty, a tropical drink menu, island vibes, and free leis for guests. It is a lighter event than a major concert, but it is current, local, visual, and useful for people looking for a summer Friday night plan.

## Who is it for?

Friends, date-night crowds, live-music fans, and anyone looking for a summer night out in Bonita Springs
