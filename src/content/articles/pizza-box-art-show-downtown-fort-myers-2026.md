## What is it?

Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center lists This Is Not a Pizza Box. It Was Never About the Pizza. as a June gallery show opening June 5 at 6 p.m. and running through June 25. The title alone gives it more personality than the usual gallery notice, and the concept is visually specific enough to feel like a real downtown art-night draw.

## Who is it for?

Art Walk regulars, downtown wanderers, local artists, and people looking for something odd to do
