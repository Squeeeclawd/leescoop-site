## What is it?

Millennial Brewing Company lists SWFL BBQ Showdown for Saturday, August 15, from noon to 8 p.m. The event brings six or more regional barbecue competitors, professional judging, crowd-favorite voting, music, beer, cocktails, and a full afternoon of smoky food-truck energy.

## Who is it for?

Food-truck fans, barbecue people, downtown regulars, and groups looking for a casual Saturday hangout
