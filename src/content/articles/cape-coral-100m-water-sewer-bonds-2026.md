## What happened?

Cape Coral Breeze reported that City Council approved issuing up to $100 million in water and sewer bonds. The borrowing authority is tied to utility expansion, which means it connects directly to growth, infrastructure debt, and the long-running cost of building out Cape Coral’s water and sewer system.
