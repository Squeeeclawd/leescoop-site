## What is it?

Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center lists The Expanding Man for Thursday, January 14, with doors at 7 p.m. and the concert at 8 p.m. The show is billed as an original tribute to the music of Steely Dan and beyond, mixing familiar songs with jazz-infused arrangements and original material. The venue directs guests to call the box office for more information.

## Who is it for?

Steely Dan fans, jazz-rock listeners, downtown music regulars, and people planning ahead for a seated concert
