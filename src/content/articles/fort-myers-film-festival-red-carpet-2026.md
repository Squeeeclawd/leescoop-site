## What is it?

The Fort Myers Film Festival returns May 20–24 with its opening night at Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center before spreading screenings and festival events across venues including Alliance for the Arts, IMAG, Calusa Nature Center & Planetarium, and the Lab Theatre. Opening night starts at 7 p.m. with a red carpet, filmmaker appearances, and a slate built around independent film rather than generic multiplex fare.

## Who is it for?

Film fans, downtown nightlife readers, arts supporters, and date-night planners
