## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers calendar lists a Culinary District Farmers Market Expansion kickoff for October 3 from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. The event notes an opening bell at 9 a.m., more than 70 returning and new vendors, and a larger footprint downtown.

## Who is it for?

Downtown shoppers, food lovers and locals looking for a Saturday market with more vendors.
