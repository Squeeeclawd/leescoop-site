## What is it?

Laboratory Theater is opening its new season with Samuel Beckett’s Waiting for Godot. Opening night is Aug. 21 at 7:30 p.m., followed by evening and matinee performances through Sept. 6. The organizer lists the production for ages 16 and older.

## Who is it for?

Ages 16 and older
