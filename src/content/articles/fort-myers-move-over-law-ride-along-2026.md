## What happened?

WINK Investigates rode along with Fort Myers police and found officers still dealing with frequent Move Over Law violations during roadside stops. Police say the failures create dangerous conditions for first responders and drivers stuck on the shoulder. The story has strong local-reader value because it mixes public safety, enforcement, and a problem many commuters see every day.
