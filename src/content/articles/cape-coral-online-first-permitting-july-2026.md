## What happened?

Cape Coral says it is moving to an online-first permitting system on July 1 for owner-builders, bringing all permit applicants into the city's digital process. Officials say the change is meant to reduce wait times and make services easier to access, with online applications, status checks, uploads, and updates available around the clock. The city says public computer stations and staff help will still be available at City Hall for people who need assistance. It is a practical local change with direct impact on homeowners trying to start repairs or projects.
