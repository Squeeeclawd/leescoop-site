## What happened?

A small plane made an emergency landing on **Bayshore Road in North Fort Myers on November 7**, striking the rear of an eastbound pickup near I-75, WGCU reported.

The Lee County Sheriff’s Office said two pilots were aboard when the aircraft experienced a mechanical issue. No injuries were reported. The FAA will lead the investigation, according to WGCU’s report.
