## What happened?

WINK reports the School District of Lee County is preparing for a projected $46.7 million deficit and has already warned teachers, parents, and students that programs and staffing could change by August. Superintendent Denise Carlin said the district overspent by $35 million and is now reviewing budgets line by line. The fight matters beyond school bureaucracy because it could reshape staffing, arts, athletics, and day-to-day school operations across the county.
