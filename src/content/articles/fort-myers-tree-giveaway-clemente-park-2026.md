## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers Parks and Recreation Department is marking Parks and Recreation Month with a 250-tree giveaway. Residents can choose up to two trees per household while supplies last, with proof of city residency required. City arborists will also be available to answer planting and care questions.

## Who is it for?

City of Fort Myers residents; proof of residency required
