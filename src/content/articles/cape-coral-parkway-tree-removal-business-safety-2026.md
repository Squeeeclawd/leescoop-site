## What happened?

WINK reports Cape Coral has started removing 33 trees and street parking along Cape Coral Parkway as part of a widening project from four lanes to six. Business owners told the station the work is already disrupting customers and could become a much bigger problem if it drags into snowbird season. Residents and merchants also raised concerns that the tighter curb-to-lane layout could create new pedestrian and crash risks.
