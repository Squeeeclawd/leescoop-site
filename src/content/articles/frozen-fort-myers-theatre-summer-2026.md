## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists Frozen at Fort Myers Theatre from June 17 through July 3, with evening performances starting at 6:30 p.m. The run gives Lee County families and musical-theater fans a recognizable summer stage option on San Carlos Boulevard.

## Who is it for?

Families, musical-theater fans, and summer visitors
