## What happened?

Gulf Coast News reports Lee County leaders are set to approve a $3,029,889.01 contract for safety improvements along a five-mile stretch of Burnt Store Road in Cape Coral. Planned changes include paved shoulders, turn lanes, rumble stripes, guardrails, signs, and fresh pavement markings, with related land acquisition for future widening. Because Burnt Store Road has a long reputation for crashes and complaints, this is a meaningful local transportation and safety move rather than routine road maintenance.
