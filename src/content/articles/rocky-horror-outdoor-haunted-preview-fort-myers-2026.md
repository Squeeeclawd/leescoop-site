## What is it?

Alliance for the Arts lists a Saturday, October 17 double feature that combines an outdoor screening of a cult classic with an exclusive preview of its new haunted maze. The event begins at 5 p.m. and includes a costume contest, dance instruction, bingo, food trucks, drinks, local vendors, and VIP options. The listing notes the movie is all ages with parental discretion, while the haunted attraction is recommended for mature audiences.

## Who is it for?

Halloween-season planners, movie fans, costume people, and adults looking for a bigger fall night out.
