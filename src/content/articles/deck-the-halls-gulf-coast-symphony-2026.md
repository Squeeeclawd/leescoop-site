## What is it?

Gulf Coast Symphony’s Deck the Halls takes the Barbara B. Mann stage on December 5 at 7:30 p.m. The holiday program includes seasonal music, the Kellyn Celtic Arts Irish Dancers and a visit from Santa. Tickets start at $59, including processing fees. Lobby doors open one hour before the performance; theater doors open 30 minutes before.
