## What happened?

WINK reports the Lee County Sheriff’s Office is adding Grappler devices and Chevy Tahoes to its fleet to make high-speed pursuits safer. The technology uses a net system to latch onto a suspect vehicle’s rear tire and force a controlled stop. Sheriff Carmine Marceno is calling it a major safety upgrade, though full fleet rollout is expected to take years.
