## What is it?

Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center lists Retro Rooftop Dance Party for Friday, July 31, from 8 p.m. to midnight. The event page promises music from the 1960s through the 1990s, costumes by decade, and a rooftop night built around dancing and nostalgia. It is one of the more visually obvious end-of-July nights out on the Fort Myers calendar.

## Who is it for?

Adults and older teens looking for a downtown night out
