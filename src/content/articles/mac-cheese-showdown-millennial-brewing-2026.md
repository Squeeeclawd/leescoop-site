## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers calendar lists Mac & Cheese Showdown at Millennial Brewing for Saturday, June 27, from noon to 8 p.m. The event puts eight chefs and food trucks into a comfort-food competition, with professional judging, music, and an all-day downtown crowd. It is free to enter and built around exactly the kind of easy local hook people can make weekend plans around.

## Who is it for?

Families, food-truck crowds, downtown Fort Myers visitors, and anyone who wants a free comfort-food showdown
