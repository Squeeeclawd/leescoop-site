## What is it?

Fort Myers Philharmonic presents The Soldier’s Tale at Alliance for the Arts on Saturday, November 14, from 2 to 3:30 p.m. General admission is listed at $50.

The Stravinsky work follows a soldier’s bargain with the devil, combining a chamber ensemble with actors and a dancer. Check the event page for tickets and current availability.

## Who is it for?

Classical-music listeners and theater audiences.
