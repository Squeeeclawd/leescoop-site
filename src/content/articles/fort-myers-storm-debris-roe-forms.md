## What happened?

The City of Fort Myers says private-road and gated communities should complete Emergency Debris Removal Right of Entry forms before storm events. The forms let city staff and contractors enter private roads after severe weather to clear debris and restore access faster. FEMA also requires submitted ROE forms to include a copy of the HOA or condominium association insurance policy.

## Why it matters

Private-road communities can slow debris removal after severe weather if access paperwork is not already on file. Completing the ROE process ahead of hurricane season can help crews respond faster after a storm.
