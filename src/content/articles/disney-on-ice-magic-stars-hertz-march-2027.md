## What is it?

Disney On Ice presents *Magic in the Stars* at **Hertz Arena in Estero March 11–14, 2027**, with seven performances featuring stories from Disney and Pixar, including *Encanto*, *Frozen 2*, *Moana* and *Toy Story*.

- **March 11–12:** 7 p.m.
- **March 13:** 11 a.m., 3 p.m. and 7 p.m.
- **March 14:** noon and 4 p.m.

The arena lists tickets **starting at $29**. Parking is **$20 plus tax and fees**, with cashless payment. Doors open one hour before each show.

Hertz Arena is at **11000 Everblades Parkway, Estero**.

## Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a local event in Lee County.
