Gulf Coast News reported that Lee County Emergency Management is urging residents to prepare before hurricane season, including practical steps like dry evacuation runs and simplified permit awareness. The timing matters because Southwest Florida is still shaped by Ian recovery and seasonal complacency is a real local hazard.
