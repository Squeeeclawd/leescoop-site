## What happened?

WINK reports that Fort Myers police arrested Mario Cabrera after officers responded to a residential fire in the 100 block of Williams Street. Police said someone intentionally started a fire outside the home while people were inside, causing significant exterior damage and damaging several items. Cabrera was taken to the Lee County Jail on a first-degree arson charge.
