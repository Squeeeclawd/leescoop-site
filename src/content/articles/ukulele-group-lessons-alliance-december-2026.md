## What is it?

The Alliance’s group lesson welcomes first-time and returning ukulele players. The hour-long Tuesday session centers on strumming and singing together. Check the organizer’s registration and instrument requirements before bringing a uke or expecting one to be provided.

[Official event details](https://www.artinlee.org/event/ukulele-group-lessons/2026-12-01/).

## Who is it for?

New and returning ukulele players interested in making music together.
