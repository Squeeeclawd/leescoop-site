## What is it?

My Way transforms Players Circle Theater into an intimate nightclub with table seating, four vocalists, and a live jazz trio. The 90-minute musical revue celebrates Frank Sinatra's songbook across multiple performances beginning August 14.

## Who is it for?

Sinatra fans, jazz lovers, musical-theater audiences, and anyone planning a polished indoor date night
