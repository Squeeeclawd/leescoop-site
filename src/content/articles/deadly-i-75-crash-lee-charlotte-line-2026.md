## What happened?

Gulf Coast News reported that one person died, one person was in custody, and several others were injured after a four-vehicle crash near the Charlotte-Lee County line. Northbound I-75 traffic was diverted at Bayshore Road, turning the crash into both a public-safety story and a major travel disruption for Lee County drivers.
