## What is it?

Visit Fort Myers lists U4ia performing June 26 from 7:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. in Bonita Springs. The act is pitched as a high-level U2 tribute built around arena-scale vocals, layered guitars, and veteran touring musicians. For readers who want a recognizable catalog show without waiting for a stadium stop, this is a solid local pull.

## Who is it for?

Classic rock fans and tribute-show audiences
