## What happened?

The crash happened near Gator Circle just before 1 a.m., with the vehicle flipping onto its side close to a home. Cape Coral police told WINK the driver fled the scene. More than 250 people in northeast Cape Coral were affected by the outage overnight.

## Why it matters

A crash that cuts power and leaves the driver missing hits both public safety and daily life, especially for nearby residents waking up to outages and unanswered questions.
