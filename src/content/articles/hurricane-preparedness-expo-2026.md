## What is it?

The City of Fort Myers says the free Hurricane Preparedness Expo 2026 will help residents get ready before storm season. It will include local experts, emergency responders, community partners, live presentations, emergency vehicle displays, activities, food trucks, and more. The event is family-friendly and runs from late morning through mid-afternoon.

## Who is it for?

Families, residents
